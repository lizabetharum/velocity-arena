06
What Endurance does

Power doesn't stay at 100%.

Your bot starts strong and fades. Endurance decides how fast it fades.

07
The rule

Here's the formula.

Power at minute t
P(t) = 100 − (20 − E) × 1.5 × t

100% at the start. Drops a steady amount every minute. The drop depends on E.

08
Try one

If E = 4 at t = 3, what's the power?

100 − (20 − 4) × 1.5 × 3   =   100 − 16 × 1.5 × 3   =   100 − 72   =   28%.

11
New words for what we just saw

You used two ideas.

Rate of change

How much the output drops per unit of input. For E = 4, the rate is −24% per minute.

Linear decay

When something decreases by the same amount every step. The graph is a straight line going down.
