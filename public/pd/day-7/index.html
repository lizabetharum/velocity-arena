05
Same formula. Two shapes.

Rename the rate of decay.

Day 2 form
The long one
P(t) = 100 − (20 − E) × 1.5 × t
Walks you through the calculation step-by-step.
Today's form
Slope-intercept
P(t) = 100 − r·t

where r = (20 − E) × 1.5
Compact. The whole rate is named: r.
06
Calculate r

For your bot, what's r?

Take your Endurance value. r = (20 − E) × 1.5

If E = 4: r = 16 × 1.5 = 24. Your bot loses 24% power per minute.

Write your r in your match data log.

07
Find your x-intercept

When does your bot die?

Set P(t) = 0 and solve for t.

For E = 4 (r = 24): 0 = 100 − 24t → t = 100 ÷ 24 ≈ 4.17 min.

Log it

Write your r and your x-intercept in the match data log. You'll predict against these numbers all week.

08
New words for what we just saw

You used two ideas.

Slope

How fast the line goes up or down — the rate of change. For your bot, that's r: how fast you lose power per minute.

X-intercept

The point where the line crosses zero. For your bot, that's the minute power runs out.

The Match Cycle.

13
If you lose

Loss recovery — three steps.

  1. Feel it. Frustration is real. Your facilitator will say so. Wait 30 seconds.
  2. Find the gap. Your model predicted X. You scored Y. Open the dashboard. Which one stat explains it?
  3. Pick one change for next round. Write it down. That's your next hypothesis.
