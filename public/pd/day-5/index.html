New words for what we just saw

You used two ideas.

Variable

A letter that changes based on your choice. Speed, Power, Endurance, and Turning are all variables.

Constraint

A rule that limits what combinations are valid. S + E + T + P = 20 is the constraint.

The Gap Sentence

Three parts. Every time.

01 · Predicted
What your formula said.
"Charge Speed 16 should kick about 80 cm."
02 · Observed
What the bot actually did.
"It kicked 62 cm."
03 · Gap
The number between them.
"Gap = −18 cm."
