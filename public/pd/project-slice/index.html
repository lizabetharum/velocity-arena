Async Tail · Built for 3 teachers with staggered starts
A face-to-face pre-pilot prep session. Then four Fridays of 1:1 check-ins anchored to each teacher's own Week N.
Pre-work (~60 min, week before the in-person prep session): read Start Here Orientation + Context Resources. Week 1 · 20 min · 1:1. Friday after Week 2 and 3 · 15 min · 1:1. A shared Field Notes doc carries 2–3 transferable insights from each call forward to teachers who started later.

Pre-Session · MakeCode Orientation

Open makecode.microbit.org on each laptop. Load the shared Velocity Arena project via QR code or URL. Plug in USB, confirm the micro:bit mounts as a drive, hit Download, watch the bot blink. No code editing — just open, connect, flash, verify.

05

Teacher Hat #2 · The Facilitator Draft

6 Protocol Cards face up. 12 Trigger Cards face down. Three rounds: each teacher drafts a protocol, picks a partner to play Maya, draws a trigger blind, and enacts the protocol while the third teacher observes. Undrafted Protocol Cards become coaching priorities. Closes with a Steal Round — name one move you want to steal from another round.

06

Phase 3 · Iteration Cycle

Match 1 → Adjust → Match 2. Write a predicted result before pressing start. Run a 3-minute match. After the match, change exactly one stat — and write the formula reason before touching the code. One change, not a rebuild. Re-run and compare.

07

Teacher Hat #3

Write 2 minutes on the exact moment allocation got hard and the one piece of information you wished you had. Discuss: what the one-change rule does to reasoning quality, and the predict-adjust-rerun loop as the engine of all 16 days.

08

Phase 4 · Critique + Present

Gallery Walk. Post stat sheet, two match cards, and one sentence on what the between-match change did. Walk the wall. Place one dot on the most mathematically interesting justification. Top 2–3 read aloud.

09

Four-Week Arc · Meta-Debrief

Dashboard Reveal — see what students open on Day 5 (Simulation Lab + Match Log). Then map today to the full 16-day arc, M–Th × 4 weeks: learned it · tested it · argued with it · taught it. Identify the hardest anticipated facilitation moment.
