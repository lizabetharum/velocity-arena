Part 1 · 20 min

Find the V · T · G triplets.

Your table has a pile of mixed cards — some words, some tables, some graphs. Each bot is described once in each form.

Your job: find one V, one T, and one G that describe the same bot.

V
Verbal
"A bot that scores 1 goal for every 2 Speed points."
T
Table
Speed 4 → 2 goals.
Speed 6 → 3 goals.
Speed 8 → 4 goals.
G
Graph
A line through (0, 0), slope 0.5.

These three are one triplet — every form says the same thing about the same bot.

Part 2 · 20 min

Add your point to the class scatter plot.

x-axis: your Speed allocation. y-axis: goals scored in yesterday's matches.

Every team adds one dot. By the end, the whole class is on the same graph.

08
Pick two points

Calculate the slope.

Slope
m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁)

Rise over run. The change in goals divided by the change in Speed.

09
Solve for the y-intercept

Use one point to find b.

Pick a point (x, y) from your scatter plot. Plug into y = mx + b. Solve for b.

Example: m = 0.5, point (4, 2).   2 = 0.5 × 4 + b  →  b = 0.

10
The point of today

Different equations.
Same data.

Whichever two points you picked, you got a slightly different m and b.

That's not a bug. That's why we use a line of best fit.

11
New words for what we just saw

You used two ideas.

Scatter plot

A graph of individual data points. Every dot is one team's match result.

Line of best fit

A single line that approximates the trend across all the dots — not through them all.
