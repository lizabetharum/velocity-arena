05
Part 1 · Pick two bots

Build the line.

  1. Pick two bots from the class data. (Same stat. Different values.)
  2. Calculate slope: m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)
  3. Solve for b using one point.
  4. Write your equation: y = mx + b
06
Your equation

Win % depends on stat.

Slope-intercept form
Win% = m · (stat) + b

Plug in any bot's stat. Get a predicted Win%. That's your model.

07
Part 2 · Test it

Plug in a third bot. Find the residual.

  1. Pick a bot you did not use to build your line.
  2. Plug their stat into your equation. Get predicted Win%.
  3. Compare to actual Win%.
  4. Residual = predicted − actual. Tells you how well your line fits the world.
08

No two teams have the
same equation.

Whichever two bots you picked, you got a different slope and a different intercept.

That's the point. The class is building many different models of the same world.

12
If you lose · pick one

Three reasons. Pick the one that explains the miss.

  1. Wrong slope. Your equation's m isn't capturing the relationship well.
  2. Wrong x estimate. You guessed the opponent's stat — and your guess was off.
  3. Ball mode flipped what wins. (Only valid in Rounds 2 and 3.)

Pick one. Write it in the data log. Then go to the next round.
