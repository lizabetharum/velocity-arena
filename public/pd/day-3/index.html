08
The Distance Formula

Here's how to measure it.

Distance between two points
d = √(( x₂ − x₁ )² + ( y₂ − y₁ )²)

Square the gaps. Add them. Take the square root.

09
Try one

From (1,1) to (4,5) — how far?

d = √((4 − 1)² + (5 − 1)²)   =   √(9 + 16)   =   √25   =   5 units.

Verify it on the grid. Walk the diagonal. Count tiles.

Same thing. Order matters: (4, 3) and (3, 4) are different spots.
