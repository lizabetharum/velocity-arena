Warm-up · 20 min

Circle Pass.

Stand in a circle. Make any sound and any movement — anything goes — then pass it to the next person. They copy it and add their own twist. Keep it moving. When you hear "reverse," flip directions.

You can not do this wrong.

04
Warm-up · 15 min

Mirror Pairs.

Face a partner. One person leads slow, deliberate movements. The other mirrors exactly. No talking. After two minutes, swap.

No winner. No evaluator. Just two people paying attention to each other.

The rule

Here's the formula.

Motor Power
Speed × 100 ÷ 20

Multiply Speed by 100. Then divide by 20.

15
New words for what we just saw

You used two ideas.

Unit rate

A fixed amount of output per 1 unit of input. 5% per Speed point is a unit rate.

Ratio

The relationship between two amounts. Speed points to motor power is the 5 to 1 ratio.
