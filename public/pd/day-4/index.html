Rule 2 — how long the hit lasts

Follow-through.

Follow-Through (milliseconds)
120 + (Power × 35)

Starts at 120 ms — even Power = 0 follows through a little. Then adds 35 ms per Power point.

12
Lock your stat card · 3 min

Power isn't a free choice.

It's whatever is left after Speed, Endurance, and Turning.

Power = 20 − Speed − Endurance − Turning

If your three already sum to 20, you have zero Power. Revise one stat before the next block.

13
New word for what we just saw

You used a new idea.

Proportional scaling

When output grows in direct lockstep with input. Double the input → double the output. Speed does this.

Non-proportional

When output grows faster than input. Double the input → quadruple the output. Power² does this.
