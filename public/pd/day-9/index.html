The 20% Gap Rule → Open the Dashboard →
10
If predicted vs. actual is more than
±20%

— that's the line. Inside it, your model is doing its job. Outside it, your model is missing a variable.

11
When the gap is over 20%

Write one sentence. Name what your model missed.

Not this

"We scored fewer goals than expected."

This

"We assumed decay rate r = 24, but the opponent's stalling cost us another 15 seconds at the floor — that's a coefficient the formula doesn't capture."

12
The thing to remember

Don't throw out the equation.

Your equation predicted X. The match gave Y. That's not a failure.

That's your model telling you which variable it's missing. Name it. That goes into the next version.
