10
In the retrospective

Four causes for a residual pattern.

Pick the one that fits your data — not the one that feels right.

  1. Slope error. Your m doesn't capture how stat translates to wins.
  2. X-variable choice. You picked the wrong stat to predict from.
  3. Model drift. Your equation was right early but the team has changed since.
  4. Environmental sensitivity. The ball mode flipped what mattered.
