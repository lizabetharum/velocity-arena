05
The formula

Watch the denominator.

Percent Change
(new − old) ÷ old × 100

You always divide by the old value. Not the new. That's the most common error.

06
The trap

Percent change ≠
percentage points.

A team raised Speed from 4 to 7.

That's 3 percentage points different. The percent change is (7 − 4) ÷ 4 × 100 = 75%.

Their bot is 75% faster. Not 3% faster.

07
The harder question

Is percent change proportional?

If your Speed goes up 50%, do your wins go up 50%? Depends on which stat changed.
