11
After every run

Did the bot do what the math said?

Compare your predicted time to your actual time.

If they match · log a confirm. If they don't · log a gap.

"Predicted 2.5 s, actual 3.1 s. Gap = +0.6 s. The Speed allocation underpredicted because ______."

Watch every team run.

13
While another team runs

Write one observation. Make it a number.

Not this
"They were fast."
No number. No claim. Doesn't tell you anything you can use.
This
"Their bot reached the ball in under 3 seconds, which suggests their Speed allocation is above 8."
A behavior. A number. A claim about the math.
