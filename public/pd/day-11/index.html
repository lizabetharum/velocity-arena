05
Phase 1 · 20 min

Take two stats. Scale each by 50%.

  1. Write the original stat value.
  2. Calculate the original output using the formula.
  3. Add 50% to the stat. Recalculate.
  4. Find the percent change in the output: (new − old) / old × 100.
06
+50% applied to two stats

Same scale. Different output.

Speed (linear)
S = 4  →  S = 6
Motor = (S × 100) / 20
Was: 20%
Now: 30%
Change: +50%
Linear: in matches out
Power (quadratic)
P = 4  →  P = 6
Charge = P²
Was: 16
Now: 36
Change: +125%
Quadratic: out runs ahead

Scale Power by 50%. Charge speed jumps
+125%

Practice Matches · 60 min

Every run is a hypothesis test.

No scoring today. Your Season 2 allocation locks Monday — today is your only chance to test it.

Format

Before each run: write the hypothesis (which stat, X → Y, expected outcome, Season 1 evidence). After: record the result and circle whether it supports or changes your proposal.

If you run without writing the hypothesis first, the run doesn't count.

13
Open Lab · Proposal Refinement · 40 min

Verify every number against the dashboard.

Two questions for every claim:

  1. Are the cited numbers accurate? Open the dashboard. Find the row.
  2. Does the rule actually help the last-place team — or just your team? Force a percent change: (new − old) / old × 100.

"It helps them" doesn't count. Show the number.
