Section 3 — Trend Line
Calculate the Slope (m)
The slope tells you how many extra goals your opponent scores for every 1 point increase in Speed.
m = (G₂ − G₁) ÷ (S₂ − S₁)
Slope Formula — Rise over Run
The slope (m) tells you the rate of change. A slope of 0.75 means: every time Speed goes up by 1, they score 0.75 more goals on average.

Section 4 — Loadout Strategy
Your Constraint & the Threat
Every bot loadout has one rule that can never be broken. You also need to name what makes your opponent dangerous.
S + E + T + P = 20
The constraint — your four stats must always sum to exactly 20
The Four Stats & Bot Formulas
Speed
Motor Power % = (S × 100) ÷ 20
Higher Speed directly increases motor power. Every extra point gives you the same boost. Unit rate
S=10 → Motor Power 50%  •  S=20 → Motor Power 100%
Endurance
P(t) = 100 − (20−E) × 1.5 × t
Higher Endurance means your robot loses battery power more slowly during the match. Linear decay
At 3 min: P(3) = 100 − (20−E) × 4.5
Turning
Arc Segments = T     Turn Ratio = 0.2 + (T × 0.02)
Higher Turning lets you make tighter turns. More arc segments means sharper control. Linear
