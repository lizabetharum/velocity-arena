Launch Sequence Designer

Launch Lab
Build & test your opening move

Design your launch sequence on its own.

The launch is your bot's first job every match: get to the ball and kick it toward the goal. This tool runs only your launch — no roaming, no match loop — so you can tune it fast. Place a ball, press a button, watch what happens, change four numbers, and try again. When it works, copy one marked block into your game code.

Setup

Open MakeCode and load the designer

  1. Open MakeCode for micro:bit
    Go to makecode.microbit.org. You do not need to sign in.
  2. New project + Cutebot Pro extension
    Click New Project and name it launch-designer. Then click the gear icon → Extensions, search cutebot pro, and add it.
  3. Switch to JavaScript
    At the top of the editor click JavaScript. Select all the default code (⌘ A / Ctrl A), delete it, then press Copy designer below and paste.
  4. Set your stats
    Near the bottom, set SPEED and POWER to your bot's values so the drive timing and kick strength match your real robot. Download to the bot.

What to change

    
The four launch numbers

    

      Tune them roughly in this order. Keep the two turn angles equal so the bot ends up facing
      the goal again after the diagonal.
    

    

      
        
VariableWhat it controlsIf it's wrong…

      
      
        

          LAUNCH_OFFSET_TURN_DEG
          How far the bot turns toward the center line at the start.
          Bot points too wide or too shallow before the diagonal.
        

        

          LAUNCH_OFFSET_DRIVE_MS
          How long the diagonal drive lasts (how far sideways it gets).
          Bot stops to the side of the ball instead of lined up with it.
        

        

          LAUNCH_SQUARE_TURN_DEG
          The turn that re-aims at the goal. Keep equal to the offset turn.
          Bot finishes pointing off-target, so the kick misses the goal.
        

        

          LAUNCH_APPROACH_MS
          How long it rolls straight forward to reach the ball.
          Too short → NO BALL; too long → it runs past / off the table.
        

      
    

    

      Mirrored corner? If your ball sits to the right of your start,
      swap LeftInPlace and RightInPlace in Steps 1 and 3 — same as you will in the game.
    

  


  
  

    
When it works

    
Move it into your game code

Copy the marked block

        

          In your tuned launch-designer project, select everything between
          COPY INTO YOUR GAME CODE — START and — END — that's your four
          LAUNCH_ variables and the whole launchSequence() function — and copy it
          (⌘ C / Ctrl C).
        

        

          Open your game project, delete your old launchSequence(), and paste yours
          in its place. If your game already has the LAUNCH_ variables, just update their numbers —
          don't paste a second let.
        

      

    


    

      Don't copy the test harness. Everything below the TEST HARNESS — DO NOT COPY
      line (checkBorder, confirmBall, chargeAndFollowThrough, the stats, etc.)
      already exists in your game code. Copying it on top would create duplicate functions and errors.
    


    

      
!

      

        
One difference from the real game

        

          The designer turns border detection off so your launch never gets interrupted on a bare
          table — that's why you supervise and catch the bot. In the full game, border safety is on, and the same
          launch will stop at the field edge. Your tuned numbers carry over unchanged.
