The Launch Sequence

Team: Names:

Your bot starts in the corner. Send it out to the midline at an angle, turn to face the ball head-on, drive up to the ball, and kick. Two drives — you work out the time for each with your own equation.

You need your Distance Lab equation, the Launch Sequence Starter (in MakeCode), a tape measure, and the ball your teacher places.

1 The plan

2 Leg 1 — corner to the midline

Turn toward the midline, then drive out to it. Measure how far the bot must travel, and use your equation to turn that distance into a drive time.

Leg 1
Turn angle: °  (from the RIGHT corner, that's LeftInPlace. 45° is a good first try; protractor for others.)
Distance corner → midline: cm
Time = ( distance − start-up ) ÷ speed = s (speed = your cm/second from the equation, not the 1–17 Speed stat)
In milliseconds: × 1000 = ms  (this is ms1)

3 Leg 2 — head-on to the ball

At the midline, turn to face the ball straight on (line up bot → ball → goal). Measure the last stretch, and find its time the same way. Aim to stop a few cm SHORT of the ball — not on top of it. The program rolls the last bit forward on its own and kicks; if Leg 2 drives into the ball, the ball moves before the kick.

Leg 2
Turn to face the ball: °  (usually the same angle, turned back the other way)
Distance midline → ball: cm
Time = ( − ) ÷ = s
In milliseconds: × 1000 = ms  (this is ms2)

Same equation both times — start-up is negative, so subtracting it adds (distance + 7 for the worked bot). Each drive has its own start-up, so do the math twice.

4 Write your launch

Four moves: turn, drive ms1, turn, drive ms2. Open the Launch Test, set your four stats, and change just your two angles and two ms values — the four highlighted numbers. Press A to run it; there's no field setup, so you can change a number and re-test in seconds.

5 Tune it — one leg at a time

Missed the midline — short or long → fix ms1 (re-check the distance ÷ speed).

Reached the midline but off to the side → fix the Leg 1 angle.

Stopped short of the ball, or drove past it → fix ms2.

Not lined up head-on → fix the Leg 2 turn.

Change one thing at a time — otherwise you can't tell which fix worked.
