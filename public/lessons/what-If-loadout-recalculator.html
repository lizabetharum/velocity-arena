Commissioner Rule Endurance ≤ 8 worked example — always fixed

Meet Team Axon. Here's their original loadout:

StatOriginal ValueRule Check
Speed (S)4—
Endurance (E)11❌ Exceeds cap of 8 by 3 points
Turning (T)3—

The problem: Endurance = 11, but the rule says Endurance ≤ 8. Team Axon must reduce Endurance by at least 3 points — and those freed points can go to any other stat(s) they choose.
Key idea: The rule says ≤ 8 — so Endurance could land at 8, 7, 6, or lower. You don't have to drop all 3 points in one place either. Spread them however you want, as long as the total stays at 20.
