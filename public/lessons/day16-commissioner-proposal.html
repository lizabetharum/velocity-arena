2-Minute Objection Time
1
A rule passes by majority vote. The Commissioner's proposal gets the required votes.
2
2-minute window opens. Any team can file a mathematical objection — but it must include a specific number and a calculation showing the % impact on formula output.
Walk through an example
Before the rule:
Turn Ratio = 0.40
Turning = 10 → 0.2 + 10×0.02 = 0.40
Is 0.40 high or low?
After the rule (Turning capped at 7):
Turn Ratio = 0.34
Turning = 7 → 0.2 + 7×0.02 = 0.34
Did the score go up or down?
The difference:
15% decrease
(0.40 − 0.34) ÷ 0.40 = 15%
Would a 15% drop hurt your team?
This is a valid objection
"Our Turn Ratio drops from 0.40 to 0.34 — that's a 15% decrease. That is our objection."

3
Objection is ruled on. The facilitator decides if the objection counts. "It's unfair" alone doesn't count — you need a number.
4
Rule stands or changes. If the objection counts, the number may be adjusted once — then it locks.

"If this rule passes, my [stat] formula output drops from [X] to [Y] — that's a [Z]% decrease."

Three Ball Modes
The Championship rotates all three. Know which stats each one rewards.
Your rule applies in all three modes. Capping Turning hurts Chaos Mode teams. Know which mode your team needs before writing your rule.
🟡
Standard Mode
Foam Ball
Foam ball. All four stats matter here. Small stat differences show up in scores.
Rewards: Speed Endurance Turning Power
Most relevant formula: Motor Power = Speed × 5
Faster bots reach the ball first.
🏓
Chaos Mode
Ping Pong Ball — Light, Fast, Unpredictable
The ball bounces off unpredictably. Quick redirects win. Turning matters more than Speed here.
Rewards: Turning ★ Speed
Key formula: Turn Ratio = 0.2 + Turning × 0.02
A Turning of 10 → Turn Ratio = 0.2 + 10×0.02 = 0.40
A Turning of 4 → Turn Ratio = 0.2 + 4×0.02 = 0.28
🎾
Heavy Touch Mode
Tennis Ball — Denser, Faster Roll, Higher Resistance
Dense ball. Power determines whether the bot can push it to the goal.
Rewards: Power ★ Endurance
Key formulas: Charge Speed = Power²  |  Follow-Through = Power × 80ms
A Power of 8 → Charge Speed = 64, Follow-Through = 640ms
A Power of 4 → Charge Speed = 16, Follow-Through = 320ms
