2 Turn the equation around

Your equation gives distance when you know the time. But the target gives you the distance and asks for the time. So solve it the other way:

To hit a target distance
time = ( target − start-up ) ÷ speed
Why that works

Start from distance = speed × time + start-up. Set the distance to your target. Undo the start-up (subtract it), then undo the speed (divide by it). What's left is the time.

Your start-up is a negative number, so subtracting it actually adds — e.g. target − (−7) = target + 7. Keep the sign and let the arithmetic handle it.
