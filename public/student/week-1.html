Day by day 5 days · 5 stats

DAY 01Speed

First Contact. Take a pre-task diagnostic — 8 questions, 12 minutes, no grade. Then run the Speed Stat Challenge: calculate motor power for Speed = 1, 3, 5, 6, 8. Then your bot takes its first drive.

DAY 02Endurance

Power doesn't last. Learn the decay equation P(t) = 100 − rt, program it into your bot, and name your bot.

DAY 03Turning

Tight or wide? Plot distance-time graphs. Program coordinate navigation with arc approximation. The math: TURNING controls how many segments your bot can arc through.

DAY 04Power

Power is a ratio. Charge speed = Power² ÷ 2. Allocate Power, run the Power Stat Challenge, build the scoop.

DAY 05Full system

20 points. Four stats. Pick one of three loadouts. Program it. Run simulation matches. Log every gap.

Using the dashboard in Week 1

Open dashboard →

It looks lean this week. That's intentional — pre-trial mode hides the Match Log until Week 2. You'll see:

  • Stat Loadout — type each stat as you allocate it. The bar fills up. Total must equal 20.
  • Simulation Lab — log every simulation round. One row per round, per formula.

A gap sentence has three parts:

Formula predicted Bot did Gap
40% motor power Reached end line in 4.4 sec 0.6 sec slower than predicted
63% power at 2 min Visibly slowed at 90 sec 30 sec earlier than predicted
Tight turning radius (T=7) Wider arc — ~20% wider Formula overestimates agility
Charge = P² = 16 (P=4) 3 power moves before depleting 1 move fewer than predicted

Write one. Add it. Repeat per round.

The View control at the top has Auto / Pre-trial / Full. Auto follows the week. Don't change it unless your facilitator says so.
