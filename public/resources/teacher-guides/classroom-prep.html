CHECKLISTClassroom & materials prep

What to set up, print, and stage.

Everything physical you need to do before students walk in on Day 1, plus the per-day print jobs across all 16 days. Worksheets go to students and teams; answer keys go only to facilitators. Print this page out and check items off as you complete them.

01 Before Day 1 — one-time setup. ~3 hrs · 1–2 days before camp

These are the four things that have to happen before students touch a bot. Do them 1–2 days before Day 1, not the morning of — the bot-training step alone takes time you won't have on opening day.

Insert batteries into the bots

One battery per bot — same direction every time, label side up. Charge the batteries the night before so you're not waiting on partial charges as students arrive.

Train every bot to recognize its lines

Each bot needs its line-following sensors calibrated to the field tape and its compass tilt-calibrated. Plan ~5 minutes per bot — for 12 bots, that's an hour. See the line-training notes in the Match Code lesson.

Charge every device

Laptops or tablets for every team, fully charged. The MakeCode editor runs in-browser — you don't need anything installed beyond a working USB connection.

Create teams of 3

Each team has three roles: Coder, Fabricator, Analyst. Roles rotate weekly from Week 2 on, so every student holds every role by Week 4. Decide the team rosters before Day 1 — not in the room — so the Day 1 launch isn't competing with cliques and conversation.

Tip Bot batteries are the single biggest source of "why isn't this working?" frustration. Plug everything in to charge the night before Day 1 and you'll skip the most common opening-day failure mode.

06 Paper math — how much you'll actually need.

A quick reference for ordering paper or scheduling time at the copier. These are rough estimates for a class of 12 students = 4 teams of 3, the program's reference cohort size. Scale linearly with your class.

For a 12-student cohort ~280 single-sided student copies across all 16 days (every-student worksheets + stat cards + recall checks + diagnostics).
~95 single-sided team copies (4 teams × the per-team worksheets that show up Days 3 onward).
~35 facilitator copies (one of each answer key, kept in your binder).
Plus visitor materials on Day 16 — sized to your guest list, not the cohort.
Don't print just-in-time Several days (6, 11, 12, 16) have multiple worksheets each. If you wait to print the morning of, you'll lose 20–30 minutes of facilitation time waiting on the copier. Pre-print the whole 16 days before Day 1, sort into labelled folders or trays, and you'll never lose a class to a paper jam.
