Gotham Tech · Mon Jul 27 – Thu Jul 30

What To Do, Monday to Thursday

Print this and keep it on the table. Do the steps in order. If you only do the steps on this page, the week works.

Set up once before Monday

4 teams: Goon Bros · The Minions · Triple T · Liquify. Both mats run at the same time. Two teams play on each mat; each team sends a driver and a ball-runner. You float between the mats and call "go."

These are the only three match-ups you ever need. Run them in order, then start over:

RoundMat 1Mat 2
1 · 4 · 7Goon Bros v The MinionsTriple T v Liquify
2 · 5 · 8Goon Bros v Triple TThe Minions v Liquify
3 · 6 · 9Goon Bros v LiquifyThe Minions v Triple T
Say this before every single match "Write two numbers. How many goals will you score? How many times will you touch the ball? No ranges. One number each. Sign it."
Say this after every single match "We predicted ____. We got ____. The gap was ____. Because ____."
The refs have one job besides "go"
Give each mat's refs a tally sheet. Count how many times each bot touches the ball. Write the number down after every match. You will need it.

Monday — get the data Jul 27

  1. 10:00Liz shows the Crosstown data. Students watch. You do not teach anything yet.
  2. 10:20Each team picks 4 stats that add up to 20. Write every team's stats on the board.These do not change again until Tuesday. Say that out loud.
  3. 10:45Run Round 1. Predictions first (two numbers). Play. Refs count touches. Then gap sentence.
  4. —Run Round 2, then Round 3. Exactly the same way.Whoever lost does Loss Recovery right away — not at the end of the day.
  5. 12:00Lunch.
  6. 1:00Run Rounds 4, 5 and 6. Same three match-ups, second time through. Same routine.
  7. 2:10Each team says their biggest gap out loud, and one reason for it. Then tell them: "Tomorrow you turn this into an equation."
Monday is the day everything else needs
Get at least 3 rounds in the dashboard. 6 is the goal. Three good rounds beat six rushed ones. Nothing else on Monday matters more than this.

Tuesday — turn it into math, then test it Jul 28

  1. 10:00Read yesterday's gaps out loud. Ask: "Whose predictions got better as the day went on?"
  2. 10:15Open Build Your Bot's Equation. Each team uses two of their own rounds from Monday. They finish with an equation.Every team's equation will be different. That is correct. Do not fix it.
  3. 11:15MODEL SWAP. Tape every equation on the wall. Each team looks at one other team's equation and writes three things: what we keep, what we change, and why.The "why" must use a number from the data. "It looks better" does not count.
  4. 11:45Each team writes this afternoon's predictions using their equation — not a guess.
  5. 12:00Lunch.
  6. 1:00Run Rounds 7, 8 and 9. Exactly like Monday.This is the most important block of the week. It is what proves their equation was worth anything.
  7. 2:10Each team fills in one card: "We changed ____ and our gap went from ____ to ____."If a team's gap got worse, that is fine. They write why. Do not fake a happy ending.

Wednesday — tests first, then the championship Jul 29

  1. 10:00Post-Task Diagnostic. Do this FIRST. 12 minutes, on their own, no talking.Say: "This is not a test of you. We are comparing you to yourself four weeks ago."
  2. 10:15Post-Reflection Survey. Same sitting. Check every name is spelled the same as Day 1.
  3. 10:30Post the bracket. Rank the 4 teams by wins from Mon and Tue.If nobody won anything, rank by smallest average gap instead.
  4. 10:45Championship. Both semis at once (#1 v #4, #2 v #3), then the final on one mat with everyone watching. Still predict before every match.
  5. 12:00Lunch.
  6. 1:00Make the Canva poster. Three things on it, nothing else: ① the equation, ② the predicted-vs-actual chart, ③ the one change they made and what it did.Add a QR code that opens their dashboard.
  7. 1:50TEACH-BACK. Team A pretends to be a visitor. Team B explains once. Team A then tries all 3 challenges with no more help.Write down exactly where Team A got stuck. Team B rewrites that one sentence. Then swap.
Do not skip steps 1 and 2 for any reason
The diagnostic and the reflection are the only things this week that cannot be done later. That is why they are at 10:00. If the day runs late, cut the poster — never these.

Thursday — show it off Jul 30

  1. 10:00Set up stations: poster, bot, grid, dashboard on a screen. One last practice of the sentence they rewrote.
  2. 10:40Exhibition. Visitors try all 3 challenges at one team's station. The team signs their card and fills the tracker.The question is: did the visitor finish all 3 after one explanation?
  3. 11:50Snack.
  4. 12:10Walk the Wall. Everyone walks past all four days of work in silence. Do not talk over it.
  5. 12:30Awards. For each award, say the number behind it: Best Prediction (smallest gap), Biggest Improvement, Best Teacher, Best Defended Model.
  6. 1:30Final journal, then done.

If this happens, do this

If…Then…
Every match ends 0–0. Nobody scores all day. Nothing is broken. Keep going. On Tuesday, use ball touches instead of goals. The equation and every step stay exactly the same.
Tell students: "The number we're predicting changed. The math didn't."
Bots still won't drive by 12:00 Monday. Stop troubleshooting. Run a Skills Ladder instead: one bot on the mat at a time, three attempts each, teams share working bots. Keep predicting and writing gap sentences. Full Plan B →
You get fewer than 3 rounds on Monday. Tuesday morning, teach the equation using the practice dataset. Then have teams redo it with whatever real numbers they have before the afternoon rounds.
Wednesday is running late. Cut the poster. Keep the teach-back. A plain poster with a rehearsed explanation beats a beautiful one nobody practised.
A team says "our equation is wrong." Good. Ask: "What number tells you that?" Then have them write the change on their Before/After card. A wrong equation they can explain is worth more than a right one they copied.
