THE BIG PICTURE
What this whole thing is
Velocity Arena is a 16-day project where students program and compete with robots while learning algebra. Here's the shift that makes it work: In the program, students genuinely encounter the need before the formula: the Launch on Day 1 gives them a bot, a 20-point budget, and no formulas. They make decisions by feel, run matches, observe gaps, and the formulas arrive to name what's already happening in the robot's behavior. The driving question — "How can you use data, equations, and code to build a robot athlete that wins on math, not luck?" — stays posted Day 1 through Day 16 precisely because the answer accumulates over time.

The pedagogy is: create the need → name the math → test it against reality → keep the gap open. The predict-adjust-rerun loop is what sustains inquiry across all 16 days.

"Every win, every loss, every draw gives students a reason to go back to their equation — to ask whether the math predicted this, and if not, why not. The formula is a hypothesis on the field, not a worksheet answer".
