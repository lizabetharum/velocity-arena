Challenge 1 Budget Allocation Constraint Reasoning
Every team starts with exactly 20 points to split across four stats. The split determines what the bot can do — and you can't have everything.
How the Budget Works
Speed (S) + Power (P) + Endurance (E) + Turning (T) = 20
Two stats directly control bot performance in matches:
Motor Power
Motor% = Speed × 5
Charge Speed

Challenge 3 Percent Change Proportional Reasoning
In Season 2, teams proposed rules that changed how stats had to be allocated. To check whether a rule was fair, they calculated percent change for every team it affected.
The Formula
Percent change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100
The denominator is always the old value — where you started, not where you ended up.
Worked example: A rule passes requiring all teams to allocate at least 5 points to Speed.
Last-place team had Speed = 2. Now needs Speed = 5.
Percent change = (5 − 2) ÷ 2 × 100 = 150%

Common Mistake
Dividing by the new value instead of the old: (5 − 4) ÷ 5 × 100 = 20%. That's wrong — the starting point is always the denominator. Team A's actual percent change is (5 − 4) ÷ 4 × 100 = 25%.
