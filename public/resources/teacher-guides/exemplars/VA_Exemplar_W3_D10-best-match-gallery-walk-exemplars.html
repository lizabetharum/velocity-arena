Marcus named the variable (Speed=5) and the result (3 goals) correctly, but wrote no formula output — this is a Level 2 card. The exit question prompt did push him to write Motor Power % = 25% — confirming the calculation isn't the barrier; the self-monitoring is. Ask: "Your card says Speed was 5. What does Motor Power % give you when you run Speed × 5? Now: what did your equation predict for goals at that Motor Power%?"
