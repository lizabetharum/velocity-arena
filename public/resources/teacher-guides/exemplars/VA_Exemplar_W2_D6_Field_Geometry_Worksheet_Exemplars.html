Teacher Look-For — Approaching (Marcus T. · Voltage)
Marcus correctly identified all field positions in Part 1 and recognized that the midpoint matches the ball's tip-off — that's a genuine conceptual win. His error is systematic throughout Parts 3 and 4: he computes the differences correctly (4−0=4, 2−0=2) but writes the raw differences into the sum instead of their squares, getting √(4+2)=√6≈2.45 every time instead of √20≈4.47. This cuts every distance answer roughly in half and carries straight into Part 4. Ask him: "In the formula, what does the little ² tell you to do to each difference before you add them?" His position choice (P2) happens to be defensible, but for numbers that are wrong.
