Part 1 — Motor Power Calculation
Speed pointsShow your workMotor Power %
Speed ALOW 2(2×100)/20 = 200/2010%
Speed BHIGH = 2×A 4(4×100)/20 = 400/2020%
Distance Measurement — 5-second run
RunSpeedDistance (cm)
A2Run 1 = 35.2   Run 2 = 36.5   avg 35.9
B4Run 1 = 90.1   Run 2 = 87.7   avg 88.9
Distance Ratio = Distance B ÷ Distance A = 88.9 / 35.9 = 2.48
After the run — was your prediction right?
Ratio 2.48, more than double. Motor power only doubled, so why does distance grow faster? At Speed 2 the bot fights friction the whole run, at Speed 4 it gets past that floor and almost all the power becomes motion. The formula is linear; the real bot has a friction floor that low Speed pays more.
Sample · pre-pilot
Teacher look-for
Same correct numbers as Jayda, but Priya predicted "more than double" up front, then explained the 2.48 with a physical mechanism: a friction floor that low Speed pays proportionally more. She separates the linear formula from the nonlinear real result — the exact distinction the program is building toward. Still in a 14-year-old's voice.
