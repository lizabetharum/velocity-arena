The Budget Constraint

Teams discover that Speed + Power + Turning + Endurance = 20 is a constraint, not an equation to solve. They build three loadouts in the simulator, then run a trade-off to see how locking Speed = 8 forces a change in another stat. The low sample adds to a balanced loadout without subtracting, breaking the sum — Marcus's recurring pattern of getting each piece right but missing the rule that ties them together. The high sample names the non-linearity in the quadratic formula. Illustrative samples, built before the pilot.
