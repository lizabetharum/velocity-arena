My Pitch — Fill this in before you rehearse
Claim 1 — Speed
Our Speed stat is 8, so our motor power is:
motorPower% = S × 5 → 8 × 5 = 40 %
Claim 2 — Counter-Loadout
We target Power (stat) because:
Charge Speed = Power² so opponents with high Power charge exponentially faster — a team with Power 8 runs at 64% charge vs our 36%. knowing their Power tells us exactly how far away they can score from
Claim 3 — Relationship Type
Circle one:
Proportional Linear (not proportional)
Because:
motorPower = Speed × 5 is proportional — it goes through origin, constant rate of change. But Goals vs Speed from Day 8 was linear NOT proportional (y = 1.5S + 1) because b ≠ 0. Two different relationships on the same card
Claim 4 — Season 1 → Season 2 Change
We changed Power from 4 to 6.
% change = ( 6 − 4 ) ÷ 4 × 100 = 50 %
We changed it because:
50% more Power = 125% more Charge Speed. old: 4² = 16%. new: 6² = 36%. change = (36−16)÷16×100 = 125%. best ROI of any stat swap we could make
