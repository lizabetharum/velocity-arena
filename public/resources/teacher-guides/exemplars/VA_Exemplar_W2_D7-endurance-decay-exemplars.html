Endurance Decay Formula:  P(t) = 100 − (20 − E) × 1.5 × t
P = power remaining (%).  E = your Endurance stat.  t = time elapsed (minutes).  Power never drops below 20%.
Part 2 — Calculate Your Decay Rate
Your decay rate is how many percent of power your bot loses each minute. Use the formula: Rate = (20 − E) × 1.5
Rate = (20 − 5) × 1.5  =  15 × 1.5  =  15% per minute

That single error propagates through every row: his table is internally consistent but uses the wrong rate, and his graph line is noticeably too flat (slope −15 vs. the correct −22.5).
