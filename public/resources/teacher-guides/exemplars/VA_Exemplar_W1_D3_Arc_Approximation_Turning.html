● Part 1 — Launch Sequence · 20 min

The ball starts at (4, 2). Your first job in every match: get your bot to the ball before your opponent. Choose a starting corner, calculate the distance, then program and measure your run.

Step 1 — Circle your starting corner
(0, 0) (0, 4) (8, 0) (8, 4)
Step 2 — Calculate your distance to the ball
d = √( (x2 − x1)2 + (y2 − y1)2 )
x₁, y₁ = your starting corner  ·  x₂, y₂ = ball position (4, 2)
Example — Corner (0, 0) → Ball (4, 2)
d = √((4−0)² + (2−0)²)
= √(16 + 4)
= √20
≈ 4.47 grid units
Your corner: (8, 0) → Ball (4, 2)
d = √((4 − 8)² + (2 − 0)²)
= √(16 + 4)
= √20
≈ 4.47 grid units
All four corners — which is closest?
Shortest distance: corner (8,0) ≈ 4.47 units
