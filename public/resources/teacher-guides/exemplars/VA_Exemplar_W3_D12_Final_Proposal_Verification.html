Final Proposal Verification — audit your own numbers

Before the Commissioner Lobby, teams verify every number in their proposal against the live dashboard, recompute the percent change the rule forces, and run a conflict-of-interest check against their own team. The low sample verifies one claim from memory and repeats the denominator error. The high sample traces every number to a dashboard location and carries the percent change through the quadratic.
