Right that A drops. The interesting part: A had the highest Speed (8) AND the highest start (51), but the lowest Endurance (2), so it fell the hardest. High Speed buys a big start, not a big finish.
2 · What does a downward slope tell you about Endurance? A flat line?
Downward slope = low Endurance; the steeper the slope, the faster the decay. Flat = high Endurance. The slope IS the decay rate.
