Teacher Look-For — Approaching (Marcus T.)
Marcus got steps 2A and 2B exactly right — Win% Change = 40 and Stat Change = 8 are both correct. His error surfaces in step 2C: he wrote 8 ÷ 40 = 0.2, swapping the two values and computing stat change over win% change instead of the other way around. This is his recurring pattern — individual formula steps are correct, but he mishandles the operation that combines them. The −26.2 residual is a clear signal something broke in the slope, not the data. Ask him: "In slope = rise ÷ run, point at the 'rise' on your table — which row did you get that from?" If he can't identify the Win% row as rise, have him sketch the two points on a graph and count up before counting across.
