Distance Formula: Shortest Path to the Goal
Can your bot move the ball far enough to score?
TeamVoltageNamesMarcus T.
The Formula
d = √( (x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)² )
Subtract first. Square each difference separately. Then add. Then take the square root.
Setup — mark B (where the ball landed after your best charge)
Step 1 — positions · Step 2 — distance B → G
A = (4, 2) given · G = (8, 2) given · B = ( 2 , 3 )
d = √( (8−2) + (2−3) ) = √( 6 + 1 ) = √7 ≈ 2.6 units
Conclusion: Easy reach. The ball is only about 2.6 units from the goal, my bot can push that.
Sample · pre-pilot
Teacher look-for
Marcus skips the squaring: he subtracts the coordinates, adds the two differences, then takes the root — √(6+1) instead of √(6²+1²). His answer (2.6) is much too small for points that are 6 apart horizontally. Nothing on the sheet flags it because he never squared. Prompt: "What does the formula tell you to do to each difference before you add? Read it left to right." Correct is √37 ≈ 6.1.
