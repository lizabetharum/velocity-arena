Part 1 · Build Your Equation 20 min
Use real match numbers from your Season 1 dashboard. Do not estimate or guess.
Step 1 · Pick 2 Real Matches From Your Dashboard

Find 2 past matches. Write the stat value (x) and the win probability (y) for each one.

MatchX — Your Stat ValueY — Win ProbabilityNotes (Optional)
Point 1 5 40 Season 1, Match 3
Point 2 8 55 Season 1, Match 7
Step 2 · Find the Slope (M)
1 — m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁) Formula
2 — m = (8 − 5) ÷ (55 − 40) Plug In
3 — m = 3 ÷ 15 Subtract
4 — m = 0.2 Divide
Step 3 · Find the Y-Intercept (B)
1 — b = y − (m × x) Formula
2 — b = 40 − (0.2 × 5) Plug In
3 — b = 40 − 1 Multiply
4 — b = 39 Subtract

Double-check: If m = 0, both matches had the same stat value — pick different matches. If your m is bigger than 10 or smaller than −10, you may have x and y switched.

Step 4 · Write Your Equation
Your Final Equation
y = 0.2 x + 39
where x = your Speed
Part 2 · Test Your Equation 20 min
Pick 3 matches you did NOT use in Part 1. Plug the stat value (x) into your equation to get a prediction. Then look up what actually happened.

Residual = predicted y − actual y. If the residual is positive, your equation guessed too high. If it's negative, your equation guessed too low.

#X (Your Stat)Y Predicted (M×X + B)Y Actual (From Dashboard)Residual (Predicted − Actual)
1 4 0.2×4 + 39 = 40 38 +2
2 6 0.2×6 + 39 = 40 42 −2
3 9 0.2×9 + 39 = 41 58 −17

Teacher Look-For — Approaching (Marcus T., Voltage)
Marcus correctly records both data points and shows clean subtraction and division in Steps 2 and 3 — but he reverses the fraction in the plug-in step, writing (x₂−x₁) ÷ (y₂−y₁) = 3÷15 = 0.2 instead of (y₂−y₁) ÷ (x₂−x₁) = 15÷3 = 5. This flattens the slope dramatically (0.2 vs. 5), so his equation barely changes with Speed — every prediction clusters around 40 regardless of x. Match 3 (x=9, residual=−17) reveals the problem clearly, but Marcus attributes it to "Speed affects wins" rather than examining whether m = 0.2 makes sense. The double-check on the worksheet doesn't flag it because 0.2 is between −10 and 10. Ask him: "If Speed goes up by 1 point, your equation says win probability goes up by 0.2%. Does that match what your dashboard actually shows?"
