Four Views Protocol — match data becomes an equation

The Week 2 centerpiece. Students pull five data points from the leaderboard (each team's Speed against its goals), plot them, fit a line, and write the equation in all four representations. The low sample inverts the slope. The high sample computes a residual and explains the team that sits off the line. Illustrative samples, built before the pilot.

Intercept 0 makes sense: 0 Speed → 0 goals. The (6,2) team is a residual: predicted 0.5×6 = 3, actual 2, so −1.
