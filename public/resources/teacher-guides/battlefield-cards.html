Session Metrics
Use these after the session to notice patterns. Numbers inform reflection — they are not a compliance score.
Metrics are reflection tools, not scoreboards. Occasional lightweight encouragement — "Nice catch," "Good one," "Clever" — is not a failure. The actual problem is praise replacing a follow-up question. If you find yourself withholding encouragement to keep a ratio clean, or forcing silence artificially, the tool is working against you. Stop tracking that number and write a note instead.
