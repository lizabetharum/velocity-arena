Velocity Arena · Match Code

Set your stats and your launch

This is the full match program — it plays a whole match on its own: it launches, roams, finds the ball, kicks, and stays inside the black border. You only change six numbers. Everything else is already written and should be left alone.

Open the program in MakeCode ↗

1 Switch to JavaScript so you can see line numbers

All the line numbers on this page are JavaScript line numbers. In MakeCode, look at the top-middle of the screen and switch from Blocks to JavaScript. Now every line has a number down the left edge.

  1. Open the program with the button above.
  2. Click JavaScript at the top.
  3. Scroll to the line number you need — or press Ctrl + G (Cmd + G on a Mac) and type the number.
Line numbers can shift. If you add or delete a line, everything below it moves. The comments are the real landmark — look for YOUR VALUE HERE and the ★ stars.

2 Your four stats — lines 1352–1355

3 Your launch — four numbers on lines 177, 181, 184, 188

Up in STUDENT ZONE B. The launch runs once, at the start of the match, and it's a two-leg path: corner → midline → ball. Turn, drive, turn, drive.

    // LEG 1 — turn LEFT, then drive forward to the midline
    CutebotPro.trolleySteering(CutebotProTurn.LeftInPlace, 45)   // ★ 1. LEG-1 ANGLE
    basic.pause(300)
    CutebotPro.stopImmediately(CutebotProMotors.ALL)
    basic.pause(150)
    if (driveForwardOrStop(1900)) { return }   // ★ 2. ms1

    // LEG 2 — turn RIGHT, then drive forward toward the ball
    CutebotPro.trolleySteering(CutebotProTurn.RightInPlace, 45)  // ★ 3. LEG-2 ANGLE
    basic.pause(300)
    CutebotPro.stopImmediately(CutebotProMotors.ALL)
    basic.pause(150)
    if (driveForwardOrStop(800)) { return }   // ★ 4. ms2
LineNumberControlsBigger number means…
177Leg-1 angle (45)How far it turns before the first driveTurns further left.
181ms1 (1900)How far it travels on leg 1Drives further out to the midline.
184Leg-2 angle (45)How far it turns before the second driveTurns further right.
188ms2 (800)How far it travels on leg 2Drives further toward the ball.

The two drive numbers are in milliseconds — thousandths of a second. 1900 means drive forward for 1.9 seconds. To go about twice as far, roughly double the number.

The two angle numbers are in degrees. Leg 1 turns left, leg 2 turns right — that's already set. You're only changing how many degrees.

Aim for behind the ball, pointed at the goal. Your kick sends the ball the way the bot is facing. Finish the launch behind the ball and lined up with the goal, and the kick does the rest.

4 Tune it one number at a time

Change one number, download, run it, watch what happens. If you change two at once and it gets worse, you won't know which one did it.

What you seeWhich number to change
Stops short of the ball, but pointed the right wayRaise ms2 (line 188)
Drives past the ballLower ms2 (line 188)
Ends up left or right of the ball, not behind itAdjust the angles (lines 177 and 184)
Doesn't get out to the midlineRaise ms1 (line 181)
Crosses the black tape and backs away mid-launchLower ms1 (line 181) — it's going too far
A distance miss is a time fix. A direction miss is an angle fix. Decide which one you're looking at before you touch a number.

5 Running it

This program calibrates before it plays, so pressing A does two different jobs:

  1. Press A — setup. It shows W: put the bot on the white floor and press B. Then B on screen: put it on the black tape and press B. Then G: point it at the goal you're shooting at and press B. It shows your stat bars.
  2. Press A again — the match starts. The launch runs, then it plays on its own for 3 minutes.
  3. B during a match is a soft reset. A+B stops the match.
If it scrolls ERR, your four stats don't add to 20. Go back to lines 1352–1355.

6 Before you call it done

  1. Do your four stats add to exactly 20?
  2. Did you calibrate white, black, and goal before starting?
  3. Does the launch finish behind the ball, pointed at the goal?
  4. Can you say what each of your four launch numbers does, in your own words?
