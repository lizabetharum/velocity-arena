Match Day
5 Core Talk Moves
Press for Evidence
"Show me the number." / "Which formula?"
Hold the Gap
Wait 7 seconds. No rephrasing. Then Turn and Talk.
Revoice
"So you're saying ___ — right?"
Turn and Talk
"Tell your partner what you'd change. 90 seconds."
Agree / Disagree
"Agree or disagree — what number supports you?"
3-Step Loss Recovery
  • Acknowledge the emotion
    "That was a tough result. Take a moment."
  • Locate one decision in data
    "Open your Match Log. Find one decision — just one."
  • Restore hypothesis-maker role
    "One change. What is it? 'We're changing _____ because _____.' "
Stop at Step 3. Don't add advice. The hypothesis belongs to the team.
Gap Sentence Structure
Predicted
Specific number
Actual
From Match Log
Gap
Computed number
"We predicted Motor% 50 would take 3 sec, actual was 5.2 sec, gap was 2.2 sec."
Missing a part? → "What was your predicted time, and what was the actual?"
Silence Reminder
1
Ask the question. Stop talking.
2
Wait 7 seconds. Don't rephrase.
3
See processing? Call on them.
4
Still nothing? Turn and Talk — not a repeat.
5
After Turn and Talk, return to the question.
Distress is different from thinking. Respond to the student if needed.
Participation Redirect
One voice dominates
Assign roles, direct to role — not to name.
"Analyst, what does the data show?"
Quiet students recording but not talking
Turn and Talk to a different partner. Debrief quiet voices first.
"I don't know, ask them"
Don't redirect. Use the Guidance Ladder — start with a question they can answer from what's visible.
"What do you see in Match Log row 2?"
Dismissive of teammates
Require a data anchor to disagree.
"You disagree — what number supports your position?"
