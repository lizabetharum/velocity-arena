Before You Start
  • Field clear, bots charged. Data logs open to 'PRACTICE HYPOTHESES — Day 11.'
  • Worksheet distributed. Hypothesis format visible on board.
  • Season 1 leaderboard posted. Last-place team's reallocation programmed.
  • Timer ready for 'Time to reach ball' recording.
  • Every team has circled their Commissioner proposal stat at worksheet top.
  • A disconfirmed hypothesis with data is more valuable than a convenient one.
Students Produce
  • At least 2 complete hypothesis entries per team
  • Timed 'Time to reach ball' (seconds) measurements
  • Conclusion: 'Supports proposal? Yes / No / Changes my proposal'
  • Commissioner Proposal: 'I propose increasing [stat] because [evidence]'
Reference
HYPOTHESIS ENTRY FORMAT
Hypothesis: "If [stat] changes from [X] to [Y], I expect [outcome] because [Season 1 evidence]."
Result: [Time: ___ sec] [Score] [Win/Loss/Draw]
Conclusion: [improved/no change/worse] + Supports? Yes/No/Changes

Say This
Open
Today is the only empirical test of your allocation before it locks. Every run is a data point that either confirms your proposal or tells you it needs to change.
Hypothesis Rule
No official scoring. Before you run, write the hypothesis. After the run, write result and conclusion. I will ask one person to read their hypothesis aloud. If they cannot state it, they are not ready.
Before Each Run
What specifically are you testing? Name the stat and direction. What value to? What do you expect, and what in Season 1 data supports that?
After Every Run
What happened? Time to reach ball? Write it now. Does it support or change your proposal? Circle the answer.
Not This
Do not tell teams which stat to change. Do not accept one confirmation as proof. Redirect to test twice or test opposite direction.
Watch For → Redirect
Team running unchanged Season 1 configAsk: 'What are you testing with this run?' If 'nothing' — ask: 'Your proposal says [stat] matters. What configuration tests that?'
Vague hypothesis (no values)Ask: 'Speed changes from what to what? What do you expect? What Season 1 data supports that?' Do not let them run until they have number, direction, and reason.
Hypothesis confirmed after one runAsk: 'Is one run enough? What if opponent had a bad run? Run a second test or test opposite direction.'
Disconfirmed hypothesis; frustrationSay: 'Write the conclusion: hypothesis not confirmed, because... That entry is your most valuable data.'
No entries after two runsStop at table. Ask: 'Walk me through what happened. I'll wait while you write it.' Return in 2 min. Do not leave until first entry exists.
Time to reach ball field skippedAt 5-min warning, prompt: 'Did you measure it?' Accept 'not timed' rather than leave blank.
Hard Stop Do not start Open Lab until at least one complete entry and the Commissioner Proposal box exist for every team. If not filled, give 2 min. Say: 'I propose increasing [stat] because [your best evidence]. Fill both blanks.'
