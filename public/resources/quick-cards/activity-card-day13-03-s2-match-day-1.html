Season 2 Match Day 1
3 of 4
Day 13 · Match-Build-Make · 90 min
MATH: Linear equations (y = mx + b) & residuals: diagnose error source
Before You Start
  • Fill Commissioner Rules from Day 12 vote record
  • Fill Prediction Market Status with each team's card
  • Confirm bracket posted; all teams have equations
  • Gather three ball types: foam (R1), dog ball (R2), rubber ball (R3)
  • Memorize three loss-recovery diagnoses
  • Before Rounds 2–3, announce ball mode and what stat it rewards.
Students Produce
  • Written prediction from equation before each match
  • Recorded match result and residual on data log
  • Diagnosis 1, 2, or 3 with adjustment strategy after each loss
  • Between-match comparison (R1 vs Season 1, or Skills prediction)
  • Exit ticket: residual sign and diagnosis with reason
Reference
THREE DIAGNOSES
1: Wrong slope (consistently off same direction)
2: Wrong x estimate (used opponent's wrong stat)
3: Ball mode changed relationship (Rounds 2–3)
BALL ANNOUNCEMENTS
R1: "Standard Mode. Equations apply as built."
R2: "Speed Ball rewards Speed—not a broken slope."
R3: "Endurance Ball rewards Endurance—not a broken slope."

Say This
Open
Season 2: your equations are live. Four things differ from Season 1. One: test your predictions. Two: ball modes reward different stats. Three: commissioner rules are binding. Four: loss recovery has three diagnoses.
Prediction Ritual
Step 1: Read your equation aloud. Step 2: Find opponent's x value. Step 3: Plug x into your equation. Write win probability now. Step 4: Show me. "I don't know" is not a number.
Loss Recovery
That result tells you something your equation missed. Let's figure out what. [Pause 5 seconds.]
Ball Mode Frame
Speed Ball. This rewards Speed—not a broken slope.
Not This
No Diagnosis 3 in Round 1. No match without written prediction. Don't skip exit ticket.

Large R2/R3 residual, small R1Model was right for Standard. Ball rewarded different stat. Is this Diagnosis 3?
Team idle between matchesCompare R1 and Season 1 residuals. Larger, smaller, or same? Write one sentence why.
