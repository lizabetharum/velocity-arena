The Budget Constraint
3 of 8
Day 5 · Launch–Explore–Summarize–Exit · 50 min
Follows Two-Formula Warm-up
MATH: S + P + T + E = 20 | P² = Charge Speed (nonlinear)
Before You Start
  • Write 2–3 predicted student responses to 'What are you giving up if Speed = 8?'
  • Read Teacher Content Note on equals-sign balance and P²
  • Write board equation: S + P + T + E = 20
  • Print exit ticket slips or prep blank sheet
  • Have backup worksheet ready
Students Produce
  • Three complete loadouts with all four stat allocations
  • Four formula outputs calculated for each loadout
  • Written explanation of trade-off if Speed = 8
  • Exit ticket with two problems showing calculations
Reference
FORMULA QUICK REFERENCE
Speed: (S × 100) ÷ 20 = Motor Power %
Endurance: 100 − (20 − E) × 1.5 = Power at t = 1 min
Turning: T | 0.2 + (T × 0.02) = Arc Segments | Turn Ratio
Power: P² | P × 200 = Charge Speed | Follow Through
CONSTRAINT: S + P + T + E = 20
Timing
0:00LaunchProject Team Volt vs. Team Blaze. P = 4 gives Charge Speed 16; P = 8 gives 64. Listen to predictions only.
0:03Task introDistribute worksheets. Name the constraint. Partner-restate: design 3 loadouts, calculate all outputs, none match personal cards.
0:08Hard stopStudents write 'Budget Constraint' page: information they have, goal. Enforce 20-second write before pen-down.
0:08Explore (20–25 min)Circulate silently first 8 min. Track Strategy 1, 2, 3. Watch for 'we give up 12 points' and totals > 20.
0:32SummarizeSelect 2 teams. Present 'Lock Speed' first, then 'Maximize Power'. Use revoicing and press-for-reasoning before revealing second Charge Speed.
0:45Misconception FixDraw a scale on board. Model: S + P + T + E on left, 20 on right. If you add 2 to Speed, something else must drop by 2.
0:50Exit TicketProblem 1: Charge Speed change when Power drops from 7 to 5 (49 → 25, decrease of 24). Problem 2: Predict Charge Speed for P = 5 vs. P = 9 when both have S = 8. Non-optional.
Say This
Open
Two teams. Same 20 points. Very different choices. Look at what each team built.
Before Design
Speed plus Power plus Turning plus Endurance equals 20. Every point you give to one stat is a point taken from the other three.
Hard Stop
Write two things: what information you have, and what you are trying to find. Do not start designing yet.
Press
Why does that work? What is it about the formula that makes the difference this large?
Not This
Do not pre-empt equals-sign errors. Note them and save for minute-45 correction.
Watch For → Redirect
'We give up 12 points'Which stat absorbs that reduction? What happens to that stat's output?
Totals more than 20Add up all four stats. What should it equal? So what needs to change?
Two loadouts with S = 8 have different Charge SpeedsWhich formula has Speed? Which has Power? Which outputs depend on Speed vs. something else?
Formula outputs left blankWhat's the formula output for Power? Show me P squared. Is 7 the output or input?
Speed = 8 trade-off answered for one loadout onlyWhat's the answer for Loadout 2? Is it the same? Why?
Team is stuckYou have 20 points. Split them four ways. What's one number you want to decide first?
