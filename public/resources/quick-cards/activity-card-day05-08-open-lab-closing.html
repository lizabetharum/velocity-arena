Day 5 · Open Lab → Closing Ritual · 35 min
Follows Simulation Matches
MATH: Formula Accuracy vs. Competition Performance
Before You Start
  • Simulation data visible on all devices.
  • Three morning loadouts on paper at each table.
  • Journal prompt written on board or as handout.
  • Physical journals or blank paper at every seat.
  • Monitoring chart printed and ready.
  • *Before class: Is inaccurate data good or bad? A loadout that matched its formula—even if it lost—tells you what to adjust. A loadout that won for unknown reasons tells you nothing.*
Students Produce
  • Comparison of two loadouts against simulation data.
  • Gap sentences identifying where predictions held or failed.
  • Season 1 allocation decision with explanation.
  • Journal entry naming one hidden competence.
  • Shared response during closing ritual (4–5 students).
Reference
MONITORING: Team | 2nd Loadout Tested | Gap Sentences | Season 1 Decision
THREE ERROR BEHAVIORS:
1. Same loadout, third run → 'What are you checking for?'
2. No gap sentences—only scores → 'Write one gap sentence, then compare.'
3. Fourth loadout, no data → 'What simulation data told you to design this?'
