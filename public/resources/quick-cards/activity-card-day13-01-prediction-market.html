MATH: Quantitative reasoning: using real Season 1 data to predict Season 2 wins
Before You Start
  • Write Season 2 match total on board
  • Have index cards and tape ready (one card per team)
  • Review each team's Season 1 record
  • Arrange teams in Commissioner's Meeting order
  • Plan which teams go first in go-around
  • *The simultaneous post is non-negotiable—it prevents teams copying each other and anchors accountability for the whole week.*
Students Produce
  • One index card per team with a single win prediction number
  • Written Season 1 data point defending the prediction
  • One-sentence verbal explanation of the data source
  • Cards posted simultaneously on wall for week-long display
Reference
WATCH: Range vs. single number | Peeking at other cards | Stuck teams with no answer
OPTIONAL TALK MOVES:
• Revoice: Confirm data-based reasoning
• Press: Quantify vague claims
• Restate: Have peers paraphrase reasoning
• Add on: Strengthen gut answers with data
Timing
0:00Set the taskRemind teams of Season 1 record, then read prediction task. Emphasize: one number, backed by real Season 1 fact, no ranges.
0:02Quiet writingThree silent minutes. Step back after first two minutes. Do not hover—looks like checking up. Redirect ranges, peeking, and stuck teams using the three watch-for prompts.
0:05Post all at oncePens down. Count to three after all teams hold cards up. Wait 30 seconds in silence—the room reads the wall. Do not fill the silence.
0:07Go around onceOpen with 'What do you notice?' (1–2 answers). Then go team by team in order. Ask each: 'What Season 1 number led to that prediction?' (one sentence). Pass on real numbers; ask one follow-up on vague answers. Do not stack questions.
0:16Name the moveAfter a data-backed answer, say: 'That move—taking a real number to predict—is quantitative reasoning. The equation next does this more precisely.'
0:18Check and goScan every card for two things: single win number + data point. If missing reason, say: 'Write your Season 1 record before you leave.' Tape all cards down. Move to Energy Reset.
Say This
Open
Season 2 starts today. Each team writes one number on your card: how many of the matches on the board you will win. One number, not a range. Base it on something real from Season 1, your win total, your win rate, or a stat you changed. You will defend that number out loud, so pick your reason now. Do not look at other teams' cards.
Silent write—range
Pick one number. Which one do you actually think?
Silent write—peeking
Write yours first.
Silent write—stuck
What was your Season 1 win total? More than that, less, or the same?
Not This
Do not skip the simultaneous post or improvise around it. Do not fill the 30-second silence after posting. Do not stack extra questions during the go-around.
Watch For → Redirect
A team writes a range instead of one numberSay: 'Pick one number. Which one do you actually think?'
A team is peeking at other teams' cards before postingSay: 'Write yours first.'
A team is stuck with nothing written after 2 minutesSay: 'What was your Season 1 win total? More than that, less, or the same?'
A team gives a vague answer ('we're stronger now') during go-aroundAsk one follow-up only: 'What was your win total, and what changed?' Take what they give and move on.
A card has a number but no written data point at checkSay: 'Write your Season 1 record on the card before you leave.'
More than 8 teams in go-around running longDrop follow-ups: one fact, next team. This is your release valve.
Hard Stop Tape every card down before moving to Energy Reset. Cards stay on wall all week.
