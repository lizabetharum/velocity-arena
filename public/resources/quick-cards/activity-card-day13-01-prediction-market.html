Day 13 · Hook · 20 min
Follows Day 12
MATH: Quantitative reasoning: using Season 1 win rate to justify Season 2 prediction
Before You Start
  • Review Season 1 leaderboard — identify highest and lowest win-rate teams
  • Check Commissioner-approved allocation changes
  • Post Y value (total Season 2 matches) on board
  • Prepare index cards and tape for simultaneous posting
  • *Simultaneous post prevents anchoring — do not skip the count of three.*
Students Produce
  • One index card with single win prediction (X of Y format)
  • Verbal or written justification citing Season 1 data
  • Posted prediction visible through end of season
Reference
DATA-ANCHORED: Cites specific Season 1 number.
TREND-BASED: References direction ("improved") without formula.
INSTINCT-ONLY: No data cited.
Timing
0:00Context & launchState format: "We will win X of Y matches." X = predicted wins, Y = total Season 2 matches. Ask what Season 1 data point the prediction is based on.
0:01Silent writingOne number only — no ranges. After 2 min, circulate using Watch For prompts. Redirect stalled teams: "What was your Season 1 win total?"
0:05Simultaneous postCount at steady pace: "One… two… three." All cards up at exact same moment prevents anchoring.
0:06Silent read30 seconds minimum. Say nothing. Let room process all predictions.
0:08Structured discussionOne per team in Commissioner's order: "What Season 1 data shaped that number?" Use revoicing for Data-Anchored, press for reasoning with Trend-Based, connecting questions between types.
0:16Exit checkEvery card must have (1) single number, (2) data point cited. If missing: 60 sec to annotate. Tape cards — they will be referenced Day 14 and Friday.
Say This
Open
Think about your Season 1 record — your win total, your losses. You will answer a question about Season 2 using that record. The answer you give now will matter at season's end.
Task
One prediction per team. Write it on your index card. Format: We will win X of Y matches. One number for X. Y is total Season 2 matches on the board. Do not look at other teams' cards.
Comprehension
Before you write — what is X? What is Y? X is your predicted win count. Y is total Season 2 matches. One number only.
Discussion
What Season 1 data shaped that number? One sentence.
Not This
Do not accept a number without evidence. Do not start the count of three until every team holds a card. Do not skip the 30-second silent read. Do not skip the exit check: each card needs a number AND a data point.
Watch For → Redirect
[W1] Team writing a rangeSAY: "Pick one number. Which do you actually think?"
[W2] Team looking at others' cardsSAY quietly: "Write yours first." Redirect once; simultaneous post protects.
[W3] Team writing zero winsDo not override. If it surfaces: "What would differ from Season 1 for one win?"
[W4] Team stalledSAY: "What was your win total in Season 1? More, less, or same?"
[D1] Cannot name Season 1 dataSAY: "What was your Season 1 win total? Is your prediction higher, lower, or same?"
[D3] Causal claim without dataSAY: "What is your actual Power stat and win rate?" Push for their own data.
Hard Stop Before writing: "What Season 1 data is this based on? Think 10 seconds." At exit: every card must have a number AND a data point — do not release until both present.
