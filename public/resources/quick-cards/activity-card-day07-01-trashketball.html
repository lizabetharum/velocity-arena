Trashketball: Warm-Up Round
1 of 5
Day 7 · Hook Block · 20 min
MATH: Order of operations (PEMDAS); Turning = 0.2 + (T × 0.02); Endurance P(t) = 100 − (20 − E) × 1.5 × t; Speed % = Speed × 5
Before You Start
  • Solve both rounds by hand to locate your hesitation points.
  • Place recycling bin in room center; stack scrap paper at each table.
  • Write scoreboard on board corner; prepare Problem 2 and Tiebreaker on notecards.
  • Designate teams of 3; confirm shooter order.
  • *Most errors are order-of-operations gaps, not formula-recall. Name the gap precisely during debrief.*
Students Produce
  • Correct Turning calculation (0.32) on team paper, all three members agreeing.
  • Correct Endurance calculation (23.5%) on individual paper.
  • Exit ticket: individual solution to Endurance = 5 at t = 2 (55%).
  • Observation of which step trips them up for Challenge Block.
Reference
ANSWER KEY
Round 1: Turning = 6 → 0.32
Round 2: Endurance = 3, t = 3 → 23.5%
Tiebreaker: Speed = 7 → 35%
COMMON ERRORS
• Adds before multiplying: 0.124 (PEMDAS)
• Skips subtraction: 90 (structural read gap)
• Writes 32% instead of 0.32 (ratio ≠ percentage)
Timing
0:00Launch & rulesWrite team names on board. Read rules aloud. Silent read of Problem 1 (10 sec), then restate. Hard stop: know what you're solving for before you write.
0:05Round 1 – ExploreStudents calculate independently. Monitor with chart. First team with correct answer (0.32) stands, shoots from seat. If stuck after 6 min, hint: 'Check what you computed first — parentheses mean multiply before add.'
0:12Debrief 1Share team that multiplied first (PEMDAS correct). Ask them to walk through steps. Press: 'Why multiply before adding?' Name the practice: order of operations.
0:15Round 2 – ExploreMore complex formula (Endurance). Write on board. Silent read (10 sec). Monitor for skipped subtraction or premature rounding. Hint if stuck: 'Check the first parentheses.'
0:20Debrief 2Share team that did subtraction first. Press: 'Why does order matter?' Restate rule in one sentence. Name: respecting mathematical structure.
0:23Exit Ticket (90 sec)Individual, no talking. Endurance = 5, power at minute 2. Scan for PEMDAS gap. Pull students aside at Challenge start.
Say This
Open
Your stat card decides who earns the shot.
Rule
Four rules. One: calculate on your own paper, then agree as a team. Two: all three stand together. Three: I check before you shoot. Four: shooter rotates every round.
Probe
What does the formula tell you first — add or multiply? What do parentheses tell you about order?
Consolidate
Order is not optional. You found which step trips you up. Now you know where to slow down.
Not This
Do not explain answers. Do not say 'you're close.' When wrong, say 'Check your order of operations.'
Watch For → Redirect
One person dominates; others copy.Ask: 'Does everyone have the same number on their paper?' If blank, sit back down. One enforcement prevents it all round.
Adds before multiplying: (0.2 + 6) × 0.02 = 0.124.Ask: 'What's inside the parentheses? What do you do first?' Let them read again.
Skips first subtraction: 20 × 1.5 × 3 = 90.Ask: 'What is inside the first parentheses?' Structural read gap, not arithmetic.
Team stands wrong; second team correct.Second team shoots. Teaches accuracy better than any debrief.
No team stands after 4 min.Deploy whole-room hint, not table help. If still stuck: 'Pens down.' Correct one in 30 sec, move on.
Shooter makes or misses; team reacts loudly.Let it happen. Frustration teaches that earning and converting are different.
Hard Stop Exit Ticket is NON-OPTIONAL. Trim Round 2 if needed, never the ticket. Individual, 90 sec, written. Use to identify PEMDAS gap for Challenge pull-aside support.
