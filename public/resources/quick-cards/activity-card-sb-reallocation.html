Say This
Open
Season 2 is open. You may move points between all four stats — your total still adds to 20. Spend each point where it buys the most.
Hard Stop
Before moving numbers: write down your Season 1 stats. Every stat. That’s your before picture for the comparison.
Press for Math
You moved a point into that stat. What happens to the output? Show me the formula.
Tradeoff
You raised that stat — which stat did the point come out of? What does that cost you?
Not This
Don't let 'stronger at everything' pass. The budget is 20 — every gain is a tradeoff. 'Which stat dropped? Show me the math.'
Watch For → Redirect
Skips formulas after sum.Say: 'Sum is right. Now show Motor Power and Charge Speed using the formulas.' No leaving without formula math visible.
Change didn't do what they predicted.Good — that's the residual. 'Predicted vs. actual: what's the gap? What would you fix?'

Sum ≠ 20.Ask: 'Add out loud. Where are you over or under?' Point to the total. Do not give arithmetic.
No prediction written.Say: 'Write one sentence: what will this change do to your output?' You'll check it in solo practice.
Exit shows 'stronger at everything' or blank formula.Note for follow-up: needs to understand the tradeoff before committing the loadout.
