Reference
FORMULA REFERENCE
Speed%: (S × 100) ÷ 20 | unit rate
Turn Ratio: 0.2 + (T × 0.02) | linear function
Charge%: P² | nonlinear
Endurance: 100 − (20−E) × 1.5 × t | linear decay
Follow-Through: 120 + (P × 35) ms | proportional scaling
Budget: S + E + T + P = 20 | ratio
ERRORS:
• Speed: forgets ÷20
• Turn Ratio: misses 0.2 y-intercept
• Power: 2P instead of P²
• Endurance: uses E instead of (20−E)

Say This
Open
Problems 1, 5, and 10 involve Speed. What's the difference between unit rate and proportional scaling?
Bridge
Checking whether a formula starts at zero is classifying by form. That's reasoning, not calculation.
Direct
If your answer exceeds 100: the formula has (20 minus E), not E. Higher Endurance = slower power loss.
Not This
Don't give formula names. Don't point to Reference. Wait for verification.
