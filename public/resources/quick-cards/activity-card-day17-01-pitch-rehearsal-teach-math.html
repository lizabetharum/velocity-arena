Pitch + Teach the Math
1 of 4
Day 17 · Launch–Tool Work–Pitch Rounds–Presentations–Exit · 60 min
MATH: Six formulas: Budget | Speed% | Turning | Endurance Decay | Power² | Percent Change
Before You Start
  • Pre-assign formulas to teams in sequence. Write assignments on board.
  • Work through one formula yourself with sample stats; write plain-language explanation.
  • Print exit ticket slips and pitch monitoring charts.
  • Post Day 17 tool URL and formula assignments before students arrive.
  • *Pitch rule: every claim must include a formula output, not just the stat value.*
Students Produce
  • Completed Pitch Tab with stat values and formula outputs
  • 2-minute team pitch delivered live, all claims numbered
  • Plain-language explanation of assigned formula
  • Individual exit ticket: one formula, one output, one sentence
Reference
PITCH RULE: Every claim needs formula output. NO: 'Speed is 10.' YES: 'Speed is 10, motor power is 50%.' POWER²: 8² = 64, not 32. ENDURANCE: Higher E = smaller loss = more power retained.
Timing
0:00LaunchTeams state one number from scouting report. Open tool Panel 0. Check: 'What makes a pitch fail?' Answer: claim with no number.
0:05Tool WorkTeams enter stat values in Pitch Tab. Run 2-min Practice timer. Watch for: stat without output, comparison without opponent number, skipped practice.
0:20Pitch Round 1One pitch per team, 2 min max. Room votes: YES=every claim had number, NO=at least one missing. One sentence feedback per claim.
0:32Teach the MathTeams complete Panel 3 for assigned formula. Plain-language checker flags jargon. Fix: drop robot words—what does this number change in real life?
0:42PresentationsTeams present in sequence. Show work from Panel 3. One follow-up question per team.
0:56Exit TicketIndividual, paper. (1) Formula. (2) Output number. (3) Real-world consequence, no robot words. Score: strong=ready, partial=flag, incomplete=priority.
Say This
Open
Defend your bot out loud with numbers. Two jobs: pitch, then teach one formula. Everything you need is in the tool.
Pitch Rule
Every claim comes from your scouting report. Open Pitch Tab with report in front of you.
After Pitch
[Claim]—YES, formula output is there. [Claim]—NO, I need the number.
Jargon Fix
Drop robot words. What does this number change in real life?
Not This
Do not swap formulas. Do not re-explain Panel 0. Do not skip exit ticket.
Watch For → Redirect
Team pitches stat value only (Speed 10, no motor power %)What does the formula give? Use that output in the claim. (Advancing)
Team compares to opponent without their numberWhat's their stat? Run it through the formula. Now use both numbers. (Visible math)
Budget used as performance claim ('fully loaded')That's legal loadout. What does it do? What output? (Probing)
Formula explained only as restatementWhat would your bot DO if that number were higher? (Reflection)
Linear reasoning on Power² ('Power 8 is twice Power 4')Power 4 = 16. Power 8 = ? Actual value? (Probing)
Endurance decay reversed (high E loses faster)Try E=18 and E=5 at t=3. Which retains more? Smaller loss = higher E. (Visible math)
Hard Stop Exit ticket is non-negotiable. Trim presentations if needed, but never skip it.
