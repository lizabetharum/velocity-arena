Before You Start
  • Set up score tracking whiteboard and visible timer
  • Load team dashboard on projector; test all devices at dashboard URL
  • Prepare one demo match row (Week 1, Opponent: Practice, Score: 2-1, W: W)
  • Write example note on screen: 'Bot slowed at 2-min mark — Endurance possibly too low.'
  • Print or display the three gameplay rules and post-match protocol checklist
  • *Notes are the data source for Week 3 decisions.* The Notes field is where engineering thinking lives, not in the score.
Students Produce
  • One complete practice match row in the Match Log
  • A note that passes the 7-day test for Week 3
  • Three-question verbal responses (Ready Check)
  • Pre-match checklist completion spoken aloud
Reference
ROLES & REFERENCE
Post-Match Protocol (4 Steps):
1. Score into dashboard first
2. Data acknowledgment sentence (stat or number, winning team first)
3. Dispute window (30 sec, evidence required)
4. Reset both bots
Match Rules:
• 3-minute match; whatever score stands
• Goal: push ball fully across far end line
• Out of bounds: placed 1 unit from border
• Both bots run simultaneously; collision is not a foul
• No mid-match code changes; stuck bot = data

Say This
Open
Think back to Week 1 simulations. You logged a gap—what the formula predicted versus what your bot did. Tell your partners one gap.
Key Frame
This week, data gets real. Every match counts toward the leaderboard. Every Note will matter when you decide stat changes in Week 3.
Notes Standard
A note tells you something useful in Week 3. Test: could you read this in 7 days and know what to change?
Rule Reframe
Once the match starts, you do not touch your bot or change code. If it gets stuck, that is your data.
Not This
Do not say 'Good game' or accept 'We won' as a note. Do not log data after the next match starts. Do not allow disputes without specific rules or numbers.
Watch For → Redirect
Team enters Week 1 for practice, then uses Week 2 for real match—dimmed dummy row appearsAsk: 'What week are we in? What does the dimming mean?' Reset and re-enter with Week 2.
Team leaves Notes blank or writes 'We won'Ask: 'What will you need to know in Week 3 to justify a stat change?' Have them rewrite with a specific stat or time.
Team starts next match before logging the previous resultState: 'Step 1: Score goes in first. Everything else second. No exceptions.' Have them backfill before continuing.
Team raises dispute but cannot point to a specific rule or numberAsk: 'Which rule applies? Which number supports this?' If no evidence: 'The call stands.'
Post-match acknowledgment is 'Good game' instead of a stat or numberSay: 'One sentence with a stat or number. What did you observe?' Rephrase until standard is met.
Team is frustrated (stuck bot, close loss, unclear rule)Say: 'Tell me what you saw. Show me where that is in the rules.' Reframe as evidence-gathering.
Hard Stop Every team must have (1) one row in Match Log, (2) a note longer than three words, and (3) Saved confirmation before rules briefing. Do not start first competitive match until all three Ready Check questions are answered and pre-match checklist is completed aloud by each team.
