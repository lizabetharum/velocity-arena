MATH: Residual = Predicted Goals − Score (Week 3 algebra foundation)
Before You Start
  • Open digital dashboard on projector with one sample row pre-filled
  • Know your answer: 'Why does Predicted Goals have its own column?' (residual = core Week 3 concept)
  • Have Column Reference table ready to display
  • Prepare to record each team's Data Owner name
  • *The Match Log is the mathematical backbone.* Every algebra concept in Week 2–4 is applied to data students generated themselves.
Students Produce
  • Labeled Match Log with all 11 columns identified
  • Team assignment of Data Owner role with name recorded
  • Confirmation of three-step entry workflow: Load, Enter, Save
  • Paper dashboard completed with sample row
Reference
COLUMN REFERENCE
1=Date | 2=Match# | 3–6=Speed/Power/Endurance/Turning | 7=Opponent | 8=Score | 9=Predicted Goals | 10=W/L/D | 11=Notes
Timing
0:00ActivationAsk: 'What's one thing to remember in three weeks?' Take 2–3 responses. Frame: 'That's data. This dashboard is where it lives.'
0:03Column WalkthroughDisplay Column Reference. Name each column. After Columns 3–6, partner restate: explain what they track and why grouped. Listen for 'variables we controlled.'
0:05Predicted GoalsAsk: 'Why its own column? Difference from Score?' Wait 5 sec. Revoice: 'The gap between prediction and actual matters in Week 3.'
0:06Completion StandardEvery cell filled same day or best estimate + flag in Notes. No blank cells. Ask: 'What happens to Week 3 analysis with blank rows?' Then teams assign Data Owner in 60 seconds.
0:08Live DemoFast demo: +Add row → fill sample row → Save → confirm. Say: 'Load. Enter. Save. Three steps.' Point to Four Views: 'Week 2 tool—needs complete log.'
0:10Exit CheckEvery team confirms: (1) Data Owner name. (2) Load, Enter, Save. (3) Why Predicted Goals is separate. Call on one student, wait 7 sec. Do not cut this.
Say This
Open
What's one thing you'd want to remember in three weeks when deciding whether to change your stats?
Key Concept
Column 9: Predicted Goals. The gap between prediction and actual—that gap is the math you'll figure out Week 3. Write it down every match.
Workflow
Load. Enter. Save. That's the real record. Paper is your backup.
Standard
By end of match day, every cell filled. If you don't have exact number, write best estimate and flag in Notes. Blank cells are not fine.
Not This
Do not skip entries or treat as filing task. Do not cut exit check. Do not fill silence during Predicted Goals question—wait 7 seconds.
Watch For → Redirect
Student asks what to do if they miss data.'Write your best estimate and note it in Notes. An estimate we can flag is more useful than nothing.'
Team cannot agree on Data Owner.Assign to team captain: 'You're the Data Owner for now. You can reassign after.'
Student finishes early and goes off-task.Redirect: 'Check your column order. Then explain why Predicted Goals is separate from Score.'
Student asks about Four Views section.'That's for Week 2. Just know it's there and needs a full log.'
Team misses distinction between independent and outcome variables.Say: 'Columns 3–6 are numbers you chose. Column 8 is what happened. What does that make Column 9?'
Hard Stop Exit Check is NON-OPTIONAL. Every team must confirm Data Owner name, three-step workflow, and why Predicted Goals is mathematically distinct. Do not move to next session until all teams pass.
