MATH: Constraint satisfaction: S+E+T+P=20. Decay Rate=(20-E)×1.5. Charge Speed=P². Turn Ratio formula.
Before You Start
  • Navigate to Facilitator Setup and enter Commissioner rule(s) exactly as stated
  • Input each team's Second Season stat allocation (must total 20)
  • Generate shareable URL and write student link on board
  • Write Commissioner rule on board for reference
  • Print or prepare monitoring chart to track student strategies
  • *Before reading the script, predict what happens if Speed goes up 3 points — check your predictions against the formulas.*
Students Produce
  • Written prediction of violation status (yes/no, point count) before opening tool
  • Completed Sections 1, 2, and 3 of Loadout Recalculator with new stat allocation
  • Exit ticket: final stat allocation (S, E, T, P=20) and one-sentence trade justification
Reference
MONITORING CHART — Track strategy type:
• Minimum-change: reduces capped stat to threshold, puts freed points in one stat
• Spread redistribution: divides freed points across 2–3 stats
• Championship-optimize: restructures entire loadout for advantage
COMMON ERRORS:
• Budget drift: freed points not redistributed; total ≠20
• Fixed transfer error: assumes all freed points go to one stat
• Decay Rate confusion: E↓ causes Decay Rate↑ (formula: (20-E)×1.5)

Say This
Open
The vote is in. The Commissioner's rule is real. Before anyone touches a computer, think about whether your loadout is affected.
Prediction
Before you touch anything — on your paper, write this: Is your team over the cap or under the floor? Yes or no. By how many points? Write your prediction now. Do NOT open the Recalculator yet.
Probe Strategy
You moved all your points to one stat. What did that do to your other formula outputs — did anything surprise you?
Advance Thinking
What would happen if you split those points between two stats instead? Would that change anything important?
Not This
Do NOT explain Power squared, Decay Rate direction, or formula patterns upfront. Let the Recalculator surface them, then ask: What do you notice?
Watch For → Redirect
Student reduces a stat but total S+E+T+P drops below 20Ask: What does your total equal right now? Remember, freed points must go somewhere to keep your budget at 20.
Student says: All freed points must go to one statAsk: Where does it say that? What if you put two points in Speed and one in Turning — does anything break?
Student lowers Endurance and is shocked Decay Rate went UPAsk: Look at the Decay Rate formula. What happens in that formula when E gets smaller? (Making math visible, not explaining.)
Student moves points to/from Power and is surprised by Charge Speed changeAsk: Why did Charge Speed change so much more than you expected? (Do not explain P² in advance.)
Student is completely stuck on the rule or violation conceptScaffold: What stat does the rule mention? What does your team currently have for that stat? Are you on the right side or wrong side of the rule right now?
Student satisfies one rule but violates another (if 2+ rules exist)Ask: Can you check every rule on the screen — not just the first one?
Hard Stop Exit Ticket is NON-OPTIONAL. Trim Explore phase if needed. Never skip. Students write final stat allocation and one justification sentence, then leave slip on desk.
