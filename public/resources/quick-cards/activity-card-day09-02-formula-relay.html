Formula Relay: All Four Types in One Race
2 of 4
Day 9 · Launch-Race-Sort-Summarize-Predict · 50 min
Follows Rivalry Card Response
MATH: All six Velocity Arena formulas — linear vs. quadratic contrast; P² quadratic growth
Before You Start
  • Solve all 20 cards; note which steps students skip
  • Set up stamp station — remain there during 22-min race
  • Post blank tally chart (6 rows, one per formula type)
  • Prepare 6 sort labels: Speed · Endurance · Turning · Power · Follow-Through · Budget
  • Write finish times on board as teams complete
  • *Budget: Step 1 finds E using S+E+T+P=20; Step 2 plugs E into Endurance. Most errors stop at Step 1.*
Students Produce
  • 20 solved formula cards with work shown
  • Error count tally by formula type
  • Prediction using target formula
Reference
FORMULA REFERENCE
Speed: Motor Power = S × 5
Endurance: P(t) = 100 − (20−E) × 1.5 × t
Turning: 0.2 + T × 0.02
Power: P²
Follow-Through: 120 + (P × 35)
Budget: S + E + T + P = 20 (find E, then use Endurance)
Timing
0:00LaunchActivate prior knowledge. Explain race rules: solve together, bring work to stamp station, max 2 attempts per card. Comprehension check: restate rule with partner.
0:05Race — 22 minCheck all three: formula written, substitution shown, answer correct. For Budget, verify both steps. Tally returned cards by type.
0:15CheckpointReview tally. If behind (~8 cards): say 'You have 7 minutes.' If early: ask for alternate solutions.
0:27Race hard stopSay: 'Time. Cards down. Pick up entire stack. Sit down.'
0:27Error Sort — 8–12 minTeams sort cards by type. Count unstamped per type. Record on worksheet. At 5 min mark call time. If 0 errors on all, assign Budget as target.
0:37Summarize — 6–10 minIdentify top 2 error types. Call on teams to explain why. For Power, surface: Speed/Endurance/Turning/Follow-Through are linear; Power is P² (quadratic).
0:47Exit TicketEach team writes prediction using target formula: (1) formula with variable names, (2) substituted values, (3) one sentence interpretation.
0:50Hard stopClosing: 'You ran all six formulas under pressure and diagnosed breakdown points. These form one system used in today's match and Season 2 inequalities.'
Say This
Open
Days 1–7 you built each formula separately. Today they appear together, shuffled. Knowing a formula when labeled differs from knowing which formula to use.
Race Rule
One person flips the card. Your team solves together. When you have answer AND work, bring it to me. I stamp if correct. If wrong, I say 'check your work.' One more attempt.
Budget Note
Budget cards are two-step: Find missing E. Then run Endurance formula with that E. Both steps must show work.
Hard Stop
Do not flip cards until I say go. Leave face down. Eyes on me. Go.
Not This
'Check your work' is only feedback. Do not leave stamp station.
Watch For → Redirect
Power: P × 2 instead of P × PSay: 'P squared means P times P. If P = 5, what is P squared? Doubling gives four times output, not two (quadratic).'
Endurance: skipped bracket in (20−E)Say: 'Calculate 20 minus E first. Then multiply by 1.5 and t.' Direct to order of operations.
Budget: E as final answer, no Endurance formulaSay: 'You found E—that's Step 1. What does the card ask you to find?' E is intermediate, not final.
Power and Follow-Through confusedHave team write both formulas side by side and label before attempting again.
Slow pace or possible copyingNote finish time and error pattern for Summarize.
Team finishes all 20 earlyAsk: 'Write second solution for two cards using different stat values.' Note finish time and hardest formula.
Hard Stop At 22 min: 'Time. Cards down. Sit down.' At 0:50: Closing—do not skip. Never skip exit ticket prediction.
