Day 5 · Transition Block · 10 min
Follows Cold Recall
MATH: Residual Analysis — gap between predicted and actual outcome
Before You Start
  • Open dashboard; confirm Auto mode with Stat Loadout and Simulation Lab only
  • Pre-fill sample loadout and one sample gap sentence for demo
  • Memorize three-part gap sentence format: Formula predicted / Bot did / Gap
  • Know answer to: 'Why log prediction before match?' (Gap shows model breakdown)
  • *Gap data needed for Week 3 prediction models. Empty Simulation Lab = no reflection answer.*
Students Produce
  • Valid stat loadout (total = 20 points)
  • One complete gap sentence in Simulation Lab
  • Team Data Owner (Analyst) assignment
Reference
GAP SENTENCE FORMAT
Formula Predicted: math prediction (stat → outcome)
Bot Did: actual outcome (same units)
Gap: difference as number. "0.6 sec slower than predicted."
Timing
0:00ContextConnect loadout to tracking. Ask: "What about performance would you remember?" Receive 2–3 observations. Frame as 'data.'
0:03Stat Loadout EntryHARD STOP: Call out stats aloud. Confirm total = 20 before proceeding. Circulate; verify each team's total = 20.
0:06Simulation Lab DemoModel one gap sentence live. Emphasize: prediction (formula), bot did (actual), gap (difference with number).
0:07Data OwnerOne person per team is Analyst. Logs Simulation Lab during rounds. Assign in 30 seconds; if stuck, assign team captain.
0:09Exit CheckThree questions: (1) Loadout total? (2) Three gap sentence parts? (3) Why log prediction, not outcome? Wait 7 sec on Q3. Press for reasoning.
