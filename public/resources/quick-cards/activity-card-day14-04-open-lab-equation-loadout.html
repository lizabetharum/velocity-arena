Day 14 · Open Lab · 35 min
Follows Season 2 Match Day 2
MATH: Residuals: actual − predicted | Flat misses → b problem; fanning misses → m problem
Before You Start
  • Post residual analysis checklist and blank table template
  • Display worked examples (flat, fanning, no pattern)
  • Review ball-mode confound from Day 13 vs Day 14
  • Prepare Skills Arena stat-change prediction template
  • *Sorting question (did direction flip?) is the gating move. Teams jumping to Step 3 misunderstand diagnosis.*
Students Produce
  • Completed residual table with Size (S/M/L) and direction
  • Yes/no: did direction flip between ball modes?
  • Revised equation (one value) OR held equation with note
  • Match Day 3 hypothesis with specific number
Reference
Size: S = 0–5 | M = 6–12 | L = 13+. Flat → change b. Fanning (S→M→L) → change m. Mixed → hold equation.
Timing
0:00Launch'Did errors point same direction in both modes, or flip?' 60 sec to organize.
0:03Steps 1–2Verify yes/no on flip question before Step 3. Gating check—reset if team jumps ahead.
0:08Step 3Ask: 'Why does that point to b [or m]?' Require one-sentence reasoning before moving forward.
0:15Step 4Change: two sentences (what data showed, why m or b). Held: name the confound.
0:20Step 5'Based on [data], I predict [number] because [change].' Push on WHY in Step 4 if ahead.
0:27Exit CheckTable filled, Step 2 yes/no, Step 4 (equation or note), Step 5 with number. Missing any = no Match Day 3.
Say This
Open
One question changes everything: did your errors point the same direction in both modes, or did they flip?
Sorting
Did direction flip between Day 13 and Day 14? Write yes or no before changing anything.
Flat
Same size every match. Why does that point to b rather than m?
Fanning
Miss grew as opponent strengthened. Why does that point to m rather than b?
Not This
Do not push teams toward diagnosis when data does not clearly point there. Held equation with reason = success.
Watch For → Redirect
Team skips Step 2, jumps to changing equationStop. Ask: 'Did direction flip? Write yes or no.' Do not approve Step 3 until answered.
Direction flipped but team wants to change equationSAY: 'The flip is the finding. Ball mode explains this. Write one sentence about which mode changed the result.'
Team wants to change both m and bSAY: 'One change only. Size pattern points to one. Pick that one.'
No clear size pattern (mixed with alternating directions)SAY: 'Path 4: no pattern. Hold equation. Use original in Step 5 prediction.' Valid outcome.
Step 4 without WHY sentenceSAY: 'Now write one sentence: why does this pattern point to that value?'
Most teams on Step 1 at 0:15Call room. SAY: 'Day 13 direction, Day 14 direction—two words each. Match or flip? 90 seconds.'
Hard Stop Exit check is non-optional: (1) table with S/M/L and yes/no, (2) Step 4 change or held note, (3) Step 5 with number, (4) one sentence explaining the evidence. Missing any = team finishes. Two minutes is enough.
