Residual Analysis and Stat Auction
4 of 4
Day 14 · Open Lab · 35 min
MATH: Residual = Actual − Predicted | Flat misses → b problem | Fanning misses → m problem
Before You Start
  • Post the five-step diagnostic workflow and residual table template.
  • Have teams pull their six match results (Day 13 and Day 14).
  • Prepare worked examples for all four paths.
  • Set up Skills Arena station for early finishers.
  • *Did the residual direction flip between modes, or hold? A flip signals ball-mode confound.*
Students Produce
  • Completed residual table with all six matches.
  • Yes/no answer: did direction flip between days?
  • Revised equation or holding note with explanation.
  • Match Day 3 hypothesis with one change explained.
  • Understanding sentence: why this pattern supports the choice.
Reference
SIZE: S = 0–5 | M = 6–12 | L = 13+
PATH LOGIC:
Flip → hold equation
Flat (same size) → b too high/low
Fanning (S→M→L) → m too shallow/steep
Mixed → hold equation
Timing
0:00LaunchDid prediction errors point the same direction in both modes or flip?
0:03Residual tableAll six matches with residuals, size categories, directions.
0:08Direction checkIf direction flipped, hold equation. If held, diagnose pattern.
0:15Diagnose sizeFlat → b problem. Fanning → m problem. Mixed → hold.
0:22Revised equation and hypothesisOne change only. Write what data showed and why.
0:27Exit checkVerify table, equation, hypothesis, understanding sentence.
Say This
Open
Did your errors point the same direction in both modes, or did they flip? Answer that first before you change anything.
Flat
Same size every match. Why does that point to b rather than m?
Fanning
Miss grew as opponent got stronger. Why does that point to m rather than b?
Not This
Do not change both m and b. Do not push diagnosis when data shows no clear pattern.
Watch For → Redirect
Team skips sorting questionAsk: 'Did direction flip? Write yes or no first.'
Direction flipped, wants to change anywaySAY: 'The flip is the finding. Hold the equation and name the confound.'
Team wants to change both m and bSAY: 'One change only. Which does the size pattern point to?'
No clear patternSAY: 'No pattern—hold the equation.' Do not push toward b or m.
Missing the WHY sentenceSAY: 'Why does this pattern point to that value rather than the other?'
Finishes before 0:20Verify all four items before Skills Arena. Clear only after you check.
Hard Stop EXIT CHECK — NON-OPTIONAL. Four items required: (1) residual table with yes/no answer; (2) revised equation or holding note with two sentences; (3) hypothesis with specific number; (4) understanding sentence. Do not wave teams through.
