Day 7 · Reveal / Open Lab · 30 min
Follows Match Day
MATH: Best Prediction = |predicted goals − actual goals| per round, averaged. Lower is better.
Before You Start
  • Calculate Best Prediction for two example teams to understand error.
  • Confirm leaderboard URL loads and displays all team rows with Wins, Goals Scored, and Best Prediction columns.
  • Verify all teams saved dashboards; flag teams showing '—' in Best Prediction.
  • Write Closing Ritual prompt on board before students arrive.
  • Best Prediction separates mathematical modeling accuracy from competitive outcome. A 0-win team with Best Prediction 0.5 has validated their math better than a 2-win team with Best Prediction 2.0.
Students Produce
  • Read team row: Wins, Goals Scored, Best Prediction rank.
  • Identify one gap between predicted and actual goals.
  • Write one math concept with full calculation.
  • Share calculation with teammate in bot-behavior terms.
Reference
LEADERBOARD COLUMNS
Wins: Match outcome.
Goals Scored: Total goals across all round 1 matches.
Best Prediction: Average |predicted − actual| per round. Lower is better.

Say This
Open
Your match data is live on the leaderboard. Before I pull up the screen, think: how many goals did you predict? How many did your bot actually score?
Explain Best Prediction
Best Prediction is your average prediction error. Smaller is better. Zero means your model was exact.
Separate Competition
Best Prediction is a separate competition tracking math accuracy, not a tiebreaker.
One Question
What are you looking at?
Not This
Do not skip explaining Best Prediction. Do not treat it as a tiebreaker. Do not ask leading questions during Open Lab.
Watch For → Redirect
Team showing Best Prediction = 0.Ask: 'What does that tell you about your allocation?'
Team trying to change stat card values.Say: 'The stat card is fixed. Code adjustments only.'
Team not engaged or avoiding data.Ask: 'What was the closest match?'
Team shows '—' in Best Prediction column.Have them check their dashboard Predicted Goals field is not blank.
Open Lab running long.Compress Closing Ritual to 90 seconds: write math concept only. Collect paper artifact.
Students conflating accuracy with wins.Revoice: 'A 0-win team with Best Prediction 0.5 validated their math better than a 2-win team with 2.0.'
Hard Stop Closing Ritual is non-optional. Trim Open Lab if needed, but collect the written artifact before students leave.
