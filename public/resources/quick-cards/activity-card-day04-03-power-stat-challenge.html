Power Stat Challenge
3 of 5
Day 4 · Launch-Explore-Summarize-Exit · 50 min
MATH: Charge Speed = Power² (squaring rule; non-linear stat)
Before You Start
  • Solve the table yourself: calculate Power 1, 2, 4, and 8 charge speeds.
  • Predict: if Power 4 = 45 cm, how far does Power 8 travel?
  • Read the Teacher Content Note on quadratic variables.
  • Do one demo run. Record your Power 4 distance as reference point.
  • *Power is the first non-linear stat. The contrast with linear stats (Days 1–3) is the core concept.*
Students Produce
  • Completed table with Power values and percentages (1%, 4%, 16%, 64%)
  • Ratio identification (1:4 holds both ways)
  • Bot test data log with Power 4 and Power 8 distances
  • Gap sentence comparing predicted vs. actual distances
  • Exit ticket: Power 6 calculation and explanation of squaring
Reference
EXPECTED RESULTS
Power 4 (16%): 8–15 cm
Power 8 (64%): 25–65 cm
Formula ratio: 1:4
COMMON ERRORS
• Doubling: Power 4 = 8% (multiply, not square)
• Can't explain the ratio
• Gap = test error assumption

Say This
Open
Speed from Days 1–3 is linear. Today's stat is different. Let's find out how.
Commit
Write your answer. One operation. Commit to it.
Setup
Floor marks are non-negotiable. Bot at TAPE 1, ball at TAPE 2.
Key Question
Look at your gap. Is it a problem—or information? [Wait. Do not fill silence.]
Not This
Do not correct directly. Ask 'Walk me through how you got that number. Now: what is 4 × 4?'
Watch For → Redirect
Doubling error (Power 4 = 8%)Ask: 'What is 4 × 4? Does that match?'
Same-ratio confusion (finds 1:4 but doesn't understand why)Ask: 'When Power doubled, by how much did charge speed change? Is that the same relationship?'
Gap = mistake assumptionAsk: 'Did you follow setup exactly?' If yes: 'The gap is information.' Name one cause.
Team stuck on tableSay: 'Power 1 = 1%. Power 4 = 16%. What did you do to 1?'
Bot won't move at 16%Increase to 18%. Note adjustment. Continue test.
