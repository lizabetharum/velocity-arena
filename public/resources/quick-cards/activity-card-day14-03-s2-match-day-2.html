Reference
RESIDUAL SIGN CONVENTION
+20 = predicted HIGH → adjust LOWER
−20 = predicted LOW → adjust HIGHER
0 = exact prediction → keep same
THREE DIAGNOSES
1. Wrong slope — same sign every round
2. Wrong x estimate — slope fine, input wrong
3. Auction drift — stat reallocation shifted model
