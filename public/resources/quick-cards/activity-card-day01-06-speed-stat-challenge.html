Speed Stat Challenge
6 of 9
Day 1 · Launch-Explore-Summarize · 40 min
MATH: Speed % = (Speed × 100) ÷ 20; Shortcut: Speed × 5
Before You Start
  • Formula posted on board
  • Answer table in hand: Speed 1=5%, 3=15%, 5=25%, 6=30%, 8=40%
  • Team pairings written on board
  • Stat cards at seats
  • Timer set to 40 minutes
  • *Do not use 'linear' today.* Contrast proportional (Speed) vs. quadratic (Power, Day 4).
Students Produce
  • Completed table of 5 motor power percentages
  • Written prediction for Speed = 10
  • Exit ticket: two quick-check problems
  • Stat card entry with allocation + percentage
Reference
Common Errors:
• Inversion: 20 ÷ Speed
• Decimal drop: 0.05 not 5%
Pattern:
Every Speed point = +5%

Say This
Open
What does 100% usually mean?
Task
The formula is asking us to... [pair-share finish].
Hard Stop
Write your prediction for Speed = 10 before you calculate.
Shortcut
Where does the 5 come from? [100 ÷ 20 = 5.] Every Speed point adds 5%.
Not This
Don't say 'linear.' Don't project answers. Don't soften the stat card decision.
Watch For → Redirect
Inversion: Speed=1 → 2000%Ask: "A motor at 2000%? Which is the part?"
Decimal: 0.05 instead of 5%Ask: "What is 0.05 × 100?"
Can't state patternPrompt three comparisons: 1→3, 5→6, 3→8. Pattern surfaces.
Speed ≥ 15 without budget awarenessAsk: "Speed=15 leaves how many points? Minimum for E+T+P?"
Quiet team passive in comparisonAddress directly: "Does your Speed=3 match? Show me."
Stuck after 5 minOne prompt: "Speed=1. What is 1 × 100?" Walk away.
Hard Stop Exit ticket is non-optional. Trim explore if needed. Never skip exit ticket.
