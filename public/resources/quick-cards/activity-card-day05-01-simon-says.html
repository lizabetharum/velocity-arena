Conditional Simon Says
1 of 8
Day 5 · Hook Block · 20 min
MATH: Speed + Power + Turning + Endurance = 20 (budget constraint)
Before You Start
  • Review 3–4 student stat cards before class.
  • Print monitoring chart (Rounds 1–5) and carry it.
  • Set up whiteboard for writing the equation in Round 5.
  • Clarify 'right answer, right reason' matters more than speed.
  • *Five rounds build from single-variable equality to four-variable constraint. Watch for boundary confusion ('>' vs '>=').*
Students Produce
  • Physical response (stand, stomp, clap, wave, cheer).
  • Verbal explanations of truth-evaluation in Rounds 1, 2, and 5.
  • Exit ticket: stat allocation satisfying two conditions, with arithmetic shown.
Reference
**Error Types:**
1. Acts on every condition
2. Never acts (doesn't know stat values)
3. Boundary confusion ('>' with '>=')
Timing
0:00Setup & card checkEstablish IF/THEN logic. Emphasize right answer + right reason.
0:03Round 1: Speed = 5Single equality. Ask why students stood or stayed.
0:05Round 2: Power > 6Inequality. Watch for boundary confusion.
0:07Round 3: Speed + Endurance = 10Two-variable equality. Count hands. Pose bridge question, wait 7–10 sec.
0:10Round 4: Power + Turning < 8Two-variable inequality. Emphasize '8 is the boundary; it doesn't qualify.'
0:12Round 5: Full constraint = 20Everyone cheers. Write equation on board. Emphasize the constraint.
0:17Consolidation & talk movesRevoice, restate, press for reasoning. Preview Day 11 systems.
0:20Exit ticketWrite allocation satisfying two conditions. 2 min max.
Say This
Open
Take out your stat card. In each round, I'll give you a condition. If true, take the action. If false, stay still. Right answer, right reason matters most.
Hard Stop
Pause, look at your card, evaluate the condition. Then act or don't.
Round 5 Reveal
That equation [write: Speed + Power + Turning + Endurance = 20] is what we're working with. You just proved it.
Boundary Clarity
Less than 8 means 7 or less — 8 is the boundary and doesn't qualify.
Not This
Do not answer the bridge question out loud. Do not fill silence before hands appear.
Watch For → Redirect
Acts every round regardless.Stop. Say: 'Look at your card — is [condition] true?' Restart.
Never acts.Before Round 3, have them read values to teammate. Check together afterward.
Boundary error (Power = 6 in Round 2).After Round 4: 'Does 8 exactly qualify for less than 8? Why not?'
Stays still in Round 5.Have them add stats aloud: 'Speed ___ plus Power ___ plus Turning ___ plus Endurance ___.'
Bridge question answered correctly.Ask: 'How did you solve that? What constraint kept you inside?'
Hard Stop Exit ticket is non-optional. Trim Rounds 1–4 if needed. Pencils down at 2 minutes; collect as transition.
