Follows Walk the Wall
MATH: 7 topics: proportional reasoning, unit rate, ratio, proportional scaling, coordinate geometry & distance, slope as rate of change, linear decay
Before You Start
  • Review 7 topic areas; identify students by growth
  • Open website, confirm diagnostic unlocked, timer visible (12-min countdown)
  • Position timer visibly; stand at side of room, not front
  • *The framing SAY is the entire intervention.* Deliver completely before any packet is touched.
Students Produce
  • Completed diagnostic packet with all 7 topic areas answered
  • Digital submission via website
  • Evidence of growth by topic
Reference
TOPIC SCORING:
• Proportional Reasoning: y = kx (constant multiplier)
• Unit Rate: rate per 1 unit, correct division order
• Ratio: compare two quantities in order
• Proportional Scaling: multiply by scale factor
• Coordinate Geometry: plot ordered pairs; use distance formula
• Slope as Rate of Change: rise ÷ run
• Linear Decay: P(t) = start − rate × t
