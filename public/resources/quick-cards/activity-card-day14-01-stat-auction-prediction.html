Stat Auction + Prediction Check
Day 14 · Hook · 25 min
MATH: motorPower% = S × 5 | P(t) = 100 − (20 − E) × 1.5 × t | Turn Ratio = 0.2 + (T × 0.02) | Output = P²
Before You Start
  • Identify last-place team; calculate one example formula output per package
  • Load HTML page; verify all 5 packages and calculator load
  • Write pre-calculated formula results on sticky note
  • Prepare tokens (3 per team, 4 for underdog), coin, auction cards, sheets
  • Post Day 13 cards on wall; have formula reference cards at every table
  • *Most common error: teams revise predictions with no data—redirect to a residual, probability, or equation output.*
Students Produce
  • One allocation decision with formula output stated aloud
  • Revised Prediction card with one dashboard number and one connecting sentence
  • Updated stat sheet showing all values sum to 20
  • Exit answer: one formula fact and one number
Reference
**Tokens:** 3 per team; 4 for last-place. **Packages:** Pkg 1 (+2 Speed): motorPower%=(S+2)×5 | Pkg 2 (+2 E): decay slows | Pkg 3 (+2 T): Ratio=0.2+(T+2)×0.02 | Pkg 4 (+2 P): (P+2)² | Pkg 5 (+3 Wild): team chooses largest gain.
Timing
0:00–0:05HTML OrientationShow roadmap. Students read panels silently. Ask 2 comprehension questions per panel.
0:05–0:07Practice BidEveryone makes fist, bids 0–3. State rule: 'Once hand opens, bid is final. No corrections, no bargaining.'
0:07–0:17Auction (5 packages)For each: (1) Announce boost, give 20 sec calc time. (2) Bid. (3) Winner states formula result aloud. (4) Update board, verify sum to 20. No result = no package.
0:17–0:22Prediction CheckRevise Day 13 card: one dashboard number AND one sentence linking to new total. If vague: 'Which residual or output supports this?' No number = original stands.
0:22–0:24Card GateCheck: original visible, revision below if changed, one number, explanation sentence. Missing = 60-sec fix before matches.
0:24–0:27WrapRead cards. Select one formula bid, revoice reasoning, ask room to restate. Name the practice: 'quantitative reasoning.'
0:27–0:30Exit CheckEach team: what stat changed and what it does—one number. If stuck, use formula card. Do not start matches until all answer.
Say This
Open
The page shows the process. We go through it once before bidding so nobody loses tokens.
Enforcement
Once your hand opens, that is your bid. No corrections, no bargaining.
Prediction Redirect
I need a number from your dashboard—a residual, equation output, or win probability. Which one supports this?
Exit Check
What stat changed and what does it do to formula output? Give me one number.
Not This
We will do better because we have more power. / Allocation does not sum to 20. / That revision sounds good without a number.
Watch For → Redirect
Team bids without calculating formula firstRequire result aloud before confirming. No result = bid goes to next bidder.
Allocation does not sum to 20Catch immediately. Do not open next package until verified.
Prediction revision has no data pointSend back. 'I need one number—residual, probability, or equation output.'
Team compares +2 Speed to +2 Power as equivalentAsk: 'Speed uses ×5, Power uses P². Are those the same?' Probe for nonlinear insight.
Exit check: team cannot state formula outputPoint to reference card. Find the formula, calculate output. Minimum 30 sec.
Hard Stop Exit Check (0:27–0:30) is non-optional. If wrap runs long, drop adding-on, not exit check. Every team must answer one formula fact and one number before their first match. Do not start until all have answered.
