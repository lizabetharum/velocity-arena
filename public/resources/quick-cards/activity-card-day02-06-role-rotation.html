Role Rotation Briefing
5 of 7
Day 2 · Make-Build-Make · 10 min
Follows Programming Endurance
MATH: P(t) = 100 − (20 − Endurance) × 1.5 × t — three roles touch it differently
Before You Start
  • Know the formula and three-role math touchpoints
  • Prepare stat cards for role assignments
  • Set timer for 5-minute decision phase
  • Understand: rotation is structure, not fairness rule
  • The three roles are a data system. When one role is inconsistent, the whole team loses ability to diagnose.
Students Produce
  • Week 2 role assignment for their team
  • One-sentence statement of role ownership
  • Role name on stat card corner
Reference
**Coder** — MakeCode, writes code, fixes breaks
**Fabricator** — Physical bot, assembly, connections
**Analyst** — Dashboard, records matches, tracks predictions
**Rotation:** Every Monday, shift one role right. By Week 4, all students hold all three.
Timing
0:00ContextReflect silently: code, bot, or data felt most natural?
0:30RolesState what each role owns. Comprehension check: 'The [role] owns the [blank]' for all three.
1:30InterdependenceWeekly shift logic: Analyst data tells Coder what to change; Fabricator setup determines accuracy.
2:30Decision (5 min)Teams decide roles. Circulate once, position to see all. Prompts only after 60 seconds.
7:30DeclarationEach team declares three names in 15 seconds. No commentary.
9:00Close + Exit CardRestate interdependence. Write role name and ownership on stat card.
10:00Hard StopBot Naming Ceremony begins. No exceptions.
Say This
Open
Which part felt most natural — code, bot, or data? [Pause 5 sec.] Starting next week, that instinct becomes a job.
Roles
Coder owns MakeCode, writes code, fixes breaks. Fabricator owns physical bot — assembly, connections, hardware ready. Analyst owns data — run dashboard, record results, track predictions vs. reality.
Connection
Analyst data tells Coder what to change. Fabricator setup determines Analyst accuracy. One role slips — the whole team feels it.
Deadlock
You have 30 seconds. The rotation means you'll hold every role before camp ends. Pick one right now.
Not This
Don't extend decision time or renegotiate roles on Day 2. Don't re-explain all three roles if confused.
Watch For → Redirect
Two want same roleAsk: 'Of the other two, which is your second choice?'
Silent after 60 secondsAsk: 'Who's been most interested in code this week?'
Going in circlesSay: 'Decide right now. You'll rotate into other roles.' Move on.
Doesn't understand AnalystClarify: 'Run dashboard, record every match, track predictions vs. reality.'
Resists assigned roleAcknowledge: 'You'll rotate into what you want. Give this one a real shot first.'
Hard Stop At 10:00, Bot Naming Ceremony begins. No exceptions. Full 40 minutes required.
