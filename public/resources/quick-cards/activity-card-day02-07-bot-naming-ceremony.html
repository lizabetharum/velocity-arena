MATH: Substitution & order of operations: a = 2x + y − 1 | b = (a + z) mod 12 | n = 3z² − x
Before You Start
  • Trace all three formulas yourself with sample values; predict where students get stuck.
  • Open Robot Team Name Generator on projector; test with your own name values.
  • Prepare exit challenge slips or have roster card backs ready.
  • *Watch for 2x vs. 2^x confusion and (3z)² vs. 3(z²) errors.*
Students Produce
  • Hand-calculated substitution steps for all three formulas.
  • Bot team name (adjective + animal + number) verified on the tool.
  • Exit challenge calculation (n = 3z² − x with x=2, y=5, z=3).
Reference
**Key Misconceptions:**
• 2x ≠ 2+x (coefficient, not addition)
• (3z)² ≠ 3(z²) (exponent applies to z only)
• Mod = remainder after dividing by 12
**Variables:**
x = letters in first name
y = birth month (1–12)
z = team number

Say This
Open
Every bot needs an identity -- one the math picks for you. Today you find out what yours is. First, you understand how the math works.
Before WE DO
We trace these formulas together on paper. I work through each one on the board. You follow along. Write your answer first, then we check together. Do not open the tool yet.
During Mod
Mod asks: what is the remainder when we divide by 12?
Naming the Move
What [Team] just did -- plugging in their numbers and following the steps in order -- is called substitution. It is a core algebra move.
Not This
Do not compute mod for them. Do not skip the exit challenge.
