Day 7 · Transition Block · 10 min
Follows Endurance Decay Equations
MATH: Speed × 100 ÷ 20 = motor power %; P(t) = 100 − rt; x-intercept = 100/r
Before You Start
  • Identify teams with incomplete stat cards
  • Match data logs open for prediction entry
  • Round 1 pairings prepared for announcement
  • Stat cards visible for reference
  • *Read-aloud tests mental model, not a warm-up.*
Students Produce
  • One signed goal prediction (single number)
  • Verbal read-aloud of formulas and equation
  • Written justification connecting prediction to allocation
Reference
TRIGGER: Move to prediction when all teams state Speed formula AND Endurance equation. RUNNING AHEAD: Ask which two stats impact goal scoring. BEHIND: Focus Speed and Endurance only.
Timing
0:00LaunchCall up the Endurance equation (x-intercept = 100/r). Frame as ceiling on bot performance.
1:00Read-aloudListen for formula + number, not numbers alone. Condition: Speed formula + Endurance equation stated before moving on.
5:00Predict + signBase on allocation. One signature = closed prediction. No ranges or revisions.
7:00Probe disconnectedIf no anchor: 'Walk me through your reasoning.' If overconfident: 'Does that fit in your time window?'
9:00CloseAnnounce pairings. Release to lunch.
Say This
Open
Your matches start this afternoon. Every team reads allocation out loud, then writes one prediction — how many goals in your first match. Go.
Model Read-Aloud
Speed: 6 × 100 ÷ 20 = 30%. Endurance: P(t) = 100 − 18t. Zero at t = 5.6 minutes.
Prediction Prompt
Write one number. Speed tells you frequency; Endurance tells you duration. Sign it. Once signed, it stays.
When Stuck
Your Speed is [X]. How fast compared to other teams?
Not This
Do not suggest numbers or complete calculations. No unsigned predictions or ranges.
Watch For → Redirect
Numbers only, no formulasPrompt: 'Read me the Endurance equation.' Do not delay group.
Speed conflated with goalsClarify: 'Speed 8 is 40% motor power. How does that affect reach?'
Prediction disconnected from dataAsk: 'Walk me through your reasoning.' If no connection: 'Does that fit your time window?'
Range instead of numberSay: 'Pick one number.' Apply 30-second countdown if needed.
Rush through read-aloudAsk for Endurance equation. If blank, give 60 seconds. Do not delay huddle.
Missing stat cardWrite current allocation, note blank, continue. Flag for Day 8.
Hard Stop At 9:00: Collect signed predictions. Announce pairings. Release to lunch.
