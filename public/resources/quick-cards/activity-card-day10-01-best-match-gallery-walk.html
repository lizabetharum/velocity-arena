MATH: Prediction gap = actual result − formula prediction; naming variables and formula outputs as evidence
Before You Start
  • Write two sample cards: Level 2 (variable named, no calculation) and Level 4 (full equation, prediction, result, gap). Post where you can point during reveal.
  • Know the Distance Formula: d = √((x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²).
  • Print and post vote criterion: 'Most mathematically interesting finding.'
  • Print and post card format: (1) Stat configuration, (2) Match result, (3) Finding with a number.
  • Carry Card Quality Observation Lens during writing and gallery walk to identify your reveal card before voting ends.
  • A finding names what produced the result, not just the result itself. 'We scored 4 goals' is a result. 'Motor Power % = 30%, which predicted 3.8 goals' is a finding.
Students Produce
  • One 4×6" index card with stat config, match result, and one finding sentence with a number
  • Written exit response self-assessing as Level 3 or 4
  • Three dot-sticker votes on cards other than their own
Reference
CARD QUALITY LEVELS
Level 1: Result only, no variable or number.
Level 2: Variable named, no formula output.
Level 3: Number and formula present, no prediction vs. result.
Level 4: Full equation, prediction, result, gap with cause.
EXAMPLE Level 4:
"Speed at 6 gave Motor Power % = 30%. Predicted 3 goals; scored 4. Gap closed when Turning dropped from 5 to 3."

Open
On your card: (1) stat configuration from your best Season 1 match, (2) match result, (3) one sentence naming what you found, with a number in it. 'Best' means mathematically useful, not biggest win.
HardStop
Before writing: open data log. Find the number. Write it on scratch paper first. Then write the sentence around it.
Level1Redirect
What number from your data log? Find it. Put it in the sentence.
Not This
Do not write 'We scored 4 goals and won.' That is a result, not a finding. A finding names what produced it—a variable, formula output, or gap between prediction and reality.
Watch For → Redirect
Result only, no variable.Which number in the data log made this result happen? Find it and write it in.
Variable named, formula not run.What does Motor Power % give you at that value? Calculate and put the number in.
0-win team: 'No best match.'Which match had the closest prediction to actual result? That is your best match—accurate predictions beat wins.
Vague card wins by large margin.Read your Level 4 card aloud. The class learns from the comparison.
