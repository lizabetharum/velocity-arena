MATH: Residual direction diagnostic: positive residuals → m too high; negative → b too low; mixed → x variable unstable
Before You Start
  • Review match records; flag all-positive, all-negative, and mixed residual patterns
  • Distribute Linear Equation Worksheets to all tables; ensure data logs accessible
  • Tape off Skills Arena half-field and post Skills Board with bot name cards
  • Post the four-step sequence visibly: Diagnose → Write diagnosis → Change one value → Write updated prediction
  • *One piece of evidence. One change. The prediction is consequential—tested against Match Day 2 results.*
Students Produce
  • Residual direction diagnosis written as a complete sentence
  • One value changed and circled on worksheet (m, b, or x variable)
  • Updated win-probability prediction recorded as a specific number
  • If early finish: prediction for one Skills Arena challenge with equation justification

Say This
Open
Today's matches gave you three data points. This Open Lab is how you use that evidence in 35 minutes.
Partner Check
Turn to your partner and complete this sentence: 'We are changing _____ because our residuals showed _____.' Say it out loud.
Two Rules
First: diagnose before you touch anything. Second: one change only. If you change two things and win, you won't know which one worked.
Mixed Direction
Write this sentence exactly: 'My residuals are mixed in direction, which means my x variable may not have a stable linear relationship with win probability.'
Not This
Do not accept vague reports like 'we made it better.' Do not clear a team for Skills Arena without verifying all three exit-check items.
Watch For → Redirect
Team skips Step 1 diagnosisSay: 'Step 1 first. Write the direction.' Return in 2 minutes.
Team wants to change two componentsSay: 'Pick one. That is evidence, not luck.'
Small residuals but wants to reviseAsk: 'What problem are you solving?' If none, redirect to calculating win probability.
Team wants to change x variableSay: 'That requires recalculating m and b—takes longer than 30 min. Change m or b instead.'
Vague wrap answer like 'we made it better'Say: 'Give me the number. What specifically changed?' Do not move on without specificity.
Skills prediction without equation groundingSay: 'What does your equation predict specifically? Put a number on it.'
Hard Stop Do not send an incomplete team into Match Day 2. At 0:35, wrap. Note incomplete teams for follow-up before Day 16. Exit Check is non-optional: all three items required (diagnosis sentence, one value changed, new prediction number) or team does not leave.
