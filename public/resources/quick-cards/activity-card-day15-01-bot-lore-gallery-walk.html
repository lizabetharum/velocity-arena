MATH: Residuals & Equation Accuracy
Before You Start
  • Check one team's Season 2 display against data dashboard.
  • Read Q2 aloud and identify which equation is most trustworthy.
  • Prepare notepad with three categories: configuration, equation accuracy, narrative.
  • Post all four gallery walk prompts on board.
  • Set up one station per team with blank sheet, pens, and sticky notes.
  • *Common error: students conflate win rate with equation accuracy. Q2 asks which equation tracked closest to actual results, not which team won most.*
Students Produce
  • Six-element wall display with bot name, configs, equation, match record, moment
  • Data log with Q1–Q4 answers for two stations visited
  • Q4 sentence: '[Bot name] started as ___, changed ___, ended as ___'
  • Exit ticket: identify trustworthy equation with wall evidence
Reference
Q2 Redirect: 'That team won a lot — but what did their equation predict? How close was the prediction to actual results?'
Gallery Walk Prompts:
Q1: Config changed most S1→S2?
Q2: Equation tracked closest to actual results?
Q3: Commissioner rule had biggest effect?
Q4: Write season story.

Say This
Open
Think about the one stat you changed most S1 to S2. That is your season story. Get it on the wall.
Q2 Check
Q2: which equation would you trust most? This is not which team won the most. What do you actually need to look at?
Q2 Redirect
Does that tell you if their equation was accurate? Look at their equation. What does it predict? Look at their record. How close is the prediction?
Closing
You've formed an opinion about which equation you trust. The Prediction Market will test it.
Not This
Do not add commentary or reactions to synthesis patterns. Read them and stop.
Watch For → Redirect
Q2 answered as 'Team A won most'Ask: 'How close was their prediction to actual results? Point to equation and match record.'
Cannot answer Q1Say: 'Calculate the difference in each stat. Which is largest?'
Stuck on Q4 narrativeSay: 'Pick three numbers. What story do they tell?'
Teams clustering, talking not readingAfter 30 sec: 'Read first. Go to another display.'
Blank wall at stationWrite one peer observation sticky note yourself.
Team missing linear equationSend to Day 13 worksheet. Equation must be labeled.
