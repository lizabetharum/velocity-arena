Percent Change: (new − old) / old × 100

Say This
Open
When you add Speed, does Motor Power go up by the same percentage? More? Less?
Task
Part 1: increase Speed by 50%, calculate Motor Power for old and new. Same for Power and Charge Speed. Decide: does each scale proportionally?
Bridge
Motor Power scales proportionally with Speed. Charge Speed does not. That distinction matters for your proposal.
Concept
Correlation ≠ causation. High Power and high wins occur together, but one doesn't cause the other without evidence.
Not This
Don't write 'difference = 15.' Use percent change: (45 − 30) / 30 × 100 = 50%.
Watch For → Redirect
Absolute difference only (e.g., '15')[M1]: Apply percent change formula to the starting value.
50% for both without computing Charge Speed[M2]: Calculate Charge Speed. Does it match 50%?
No rule in Section 1[L1]: Pick stat, direction (CAP/FLOOR), number 1–18. Write all three now.
Section 2 has stat values, no formula[L2]: Plug a team's stat into the formula. Write stat, formula, output.
Causal language ('causes wins')Flag and revise to 'is associated with.' What other stat could explain this?
Cites other team's dataEncouraged. Name which team's data in Section 2.
