Open Lab: Data Swap + Post-Match Debrief
5 of 5
Day 9 · Explore-Summarize-Exit · 40 min
MATH: Pattern identification → testable hypothesis
Before You Start
  • Write your own answer: what pattern would you look for?
  • Prepare partner pairings; ensure each team receives a different dashboard.
  • Set up visible 10-minute timer and index cards for findings.
  • Review monitoring chart with finding types and watch-for triggers.
  • Plan three-team share sequence using concrete → abstract progression.
  • *Push for the layer underneath every result. Pattern: 'goal rate dropped despite constant Speed'; Result: 'they lost.'*
Students Produce
  • One finding card naming specific pattern, metric, and implication.
  • Exit ticket: one-sentence finding with metric, match number, evidence.
  • Exit ticket: Hypothesis using 'If [stat] changes, expect [outcome] because [data]'.
  • Journal note during Open Lab Phase 2.
Reference
**Finding Types:**
• Stat-outcome correlation
• Match trend (across 3 days)
• Cross-metric comparison
**Watch For:**
• Result statement (no pattern)
• Advice (no evidence)
• Single-match focus

Say This
Open
Someone spotting a problem you missed in 10 seconds—not because they're smarter, but because they didn't make it. That's today.
Task
10 minutes to find one pattern—not a result, not advice. Write: 'We noticed [pattern]. This matters because [implication].'
Circulate
You connected [stat] to [outcome]. Does that hold across all three matches, or does it change?
Closing
Data findings are specific: metric, match, number. 'Goal rate: 4→1, Speed: 8 constant' tells you what constrains them.
Not This
Do not accept 'they lost' or 'change Power' without evidence. Push: 'Which number? Where in the dashboard?'
Watch For → Redirect
Result instead of patternAsk: 'What in the data explains why? Which number changed?'
Advice without evidenceAsk: 'Show me the number that supports that.'
One match only, not pattern across timeAsk: 'What stayed constant? Does that explain the change?'
Two stats but cannot prioritizeAsk: 'Change only one—which has more leverage?'
Stuck; staring without engagementPoint to match log: 'Start here. One row. Say it out loud.'
Vague exit ticket or missing structureFlag. Add to Day 10 for redirect before hypothesis work.
