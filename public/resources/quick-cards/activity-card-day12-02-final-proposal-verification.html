Follows Lobby the Commissioners
MATH: Percent change: (after − before) ÷ before × 100
Before You Start
  • Write five concept types from memory: proportional scaling, unit rate, ratio, slope as rate of change, linear decay.
  • Read Teacher Content Note on percent change formula and common errors.
  • Note which teams revised proposals during Lobby.
  • Ensure dashboards are open on all devices or printed.
  • Have paper Presentation Worksheets distributed.
  • *Most common error: dividing by 'after' instead of 'before'.*
Students Produce
  • Verified data points on dashboard (Section 1).
  • Calculated percent change for last-place and own team (Section 2).
  • Confirmed no conflict of interest (Section 3).
  • Named concept type (Section 4).
  • Copied items 1–5 to Presentation Worksheet.
Reference
Five Concept Types:
• Proportional scaling: constant multiplier
• Unit rate: amount per one unit
• Ratio: part-to-part or part-to-whole
• Slope as rate of change: change relative to variable
• Linear decay: constant decrease
Edge Case: If last-place = 0, percent change undefined.

Say This
Open
Make sure every number you cite is one you can find on the dashboard. If you can't point to it, it doesn't count.
Hard Stop
Pick a Section 1 captain and Section 2 captain right now. Ten seconds. Go.
Accountability
One person per team answers from memory—no device. How did you calculate this number?
Naming
What just happened is percent change and proportional scaling. Those are what you'll name in the Commissioner's Meeting.
Not This
Do not give answers during circulation. Do not wait for every team to finish before accountability check.
Watch For → Redirect
Absolute values only ('3 vs 2')Ask: 'Out of what starting value each?' Guide to percent change.
Inverted formula: ÷ afterAsk: 'Which value is the starting point?' Point to formula; step back.
Proposing team benefits moreSay: 'Revise now. What would the rule need to flip these percentages?'
Wrong concept labelAsk: 'Does your rule change by amount or multiply by ratio? Walk through which you're doing.'
Unverified number citedAsk: 'Point to it in the dashboard.' If not found: 'Use verified number instead.'
Last-place value = 0Percent change undefined. Use absolute change instead.
