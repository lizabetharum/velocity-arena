Reference
**Sign flip error:** P(1)=121 means added instead of subtracted.
**16 × 1.5 error:** Student writes 16.5 instead of 24 (additive vs. multiplicative).
**Decay rate formula:** (20 − E) × 1.5
**Floor rule:** P(t) clamped at 20%
**LO 2.1:** Slope = battery drops X% per minute
**LO 2.2:** Y-intercept (100%) and slope in formula
