Before You Start
  • Write sentence frame on every index card before class
  • Know all three formulas cold; practice teaching goal card for each
  • Verify all five station items in place: bot, grid, report, dashboard, formula sheet
  • Confirm visitor arrival scheduled after 9:25 AM
  • Charge all bots; keep powered off until visitor use
  • *Frame teaching as helping visitor understand one concept, not performing.*
Students Produce
  • One completed teaching goal card with specific number and because-clause
  • A ready station with all five items positioned
  • Peer practice round teaching all three tasks to teammate
Reference
TEACHING GOAL CARD RUBRIC
✅ Strong: Specific number + mechanism
⚠️ Partial: Number without mechanism
❌ Vague: No number, no concept
THREE VISITOR TASKS
1. Physical Handoff: Place dot, hand controller
2. Coordinate Distance: Plot two points, count together, show formula
3. One Number, One Sentence: Show card; visitor restates
Timing
0:00OpeningReframe from 'perform' to 'help understand.' Your job is not to impress them—help them understand one thing.
3:00SetupDirect teams to stations. Verify all five items at every station before Phase 3.
10:00ComprehensionStudents state the three visitor tasks to partner. Do not release until all teams confirm.
10:00Peer PracticeRead opening line: 'This is our bot. It runs at [number] percent motor power. Want to see it move?' Each teaches once in 9 minutes.
14:30Role SwitchVisitor becomes teacher. Continue circulation, prioritize struggling stations.
19:00DebriefName hardest moment from monitoring. Give recovery move: use opening line as reset. Name practice: precision of mathematical language.
22:00Goal CardComplete both blanks. First blank: specific number or concept. Because-clause: mechanism. Post at station.
24:00Final CheckConfirm all five items, card posted, students ready, bot accessible. Open door silently.
Say This
Open
Your job today is not to impress them. Your job is to help them understand one thing. Just one.
Task
Here are the three things a visitor does. One is the teacher. One is the visitor. Every teacher says this first: 'This is our bot. It runs at [your number] percent motor power. Want to see it move?' After that, go.
Debrief
If a visitor gets stuck, use the opening line again and start over. The bot is your reset button. You always have somewhere to go.
Card
First blank needs a number or concept by name. Something specific. Two minutes. Complete the card. Post it at the front of your station, visible to a standing visitor.
Not This
Do not say: 'Are you ready to teach?' Do not skip the debrief. Do not delay Phase 3 past 11:00. Do not let a bot run during setup. Do not write goal cards for students.
Watch For → Redirect
Student skips Task 1, goes straight to gridSay: 'Start with the bot. Give them the controller first.'
Teacher does the counting in Task 2Say: 'Count together—you point, they count.'
Teacher reads Task 3 card, visitor silentSay: 'Ask them: Can you say that back in your own words?'
Student goes silent after opening lineSay quietly: 'Use your opening line. Want to see it move? Step back.'
Student uses shortcut without explaining whySay: 'What does the 20 represent? Why 20?'
Goal card has number but no because-clauseSay: 'What does that number actually do? Why does it matter?'
Hard Stop Do not move to Phase 3 until all teams state the three visitor tasks. Do not skip the 19:00 debrief. Do not delay visitor arrival for one unverified station.
