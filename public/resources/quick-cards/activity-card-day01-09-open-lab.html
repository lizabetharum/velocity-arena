MATH: Autonomous decision-making & hypothesis formation
Before You Start
  • Practice 10-second silence with a colleague
  • Ensure bots powered, MakeCode open, coordinate grids on floor
  • Data dashboards accessible but optional
  • Bring Watch For chart to circulation
  • *The hardest move is silence. Do not answer 'What should we work toward?'—the absence of your answer is the lesson.*
Students Produce
  • Self-initiated bot decision (not prompted)
  • Exploration of Speed variable
  • Individual reflection on one surprise
Reference
SCRIPTED QUESTIONS:
• "What are you trying?" → Listen, nod, move on.
• "What do you want the bot to do right now?" [pause 10 sec] → "Try that."
• "What did you expect it to do?" → Frustrated students only.
