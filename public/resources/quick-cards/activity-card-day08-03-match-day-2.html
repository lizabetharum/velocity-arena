Before You Start
  • Post Day 8 round-robin bracket (two rounds, visible from all seats)
  • Fill scatter plot equation section in match data logs from morning activity
  • Set up prediction-accuracy tracking chart (Page 6) for both rounds
  • Verify match field ready: grid, goals, start positions; timer charged
  • Stage Rivalry Cards materials for transition; stage leaderboard from Day 7
  • *Key distinction: revising the PREDICTION for Round 2 is correct. Revising the EQUATION itself only happens in Open Lab with more data.*
Students Produce
  • Written scatter plot equation prediction before each match
  • Post-match data log entry with W/L and revised prediction if needed
  • Exit ticket: one sentence explaining prediction accuracy and why
  • Dashboard after each match showing prediction gap

LOSS RECOVERY (Steps 1–2 only)
Step 1: Acknowledge frustration (30 sec)
Step 2: Compare prediction vs. actual. Ask: 'What Speed did you plug in? What did you run?'
EXIT TICKET PROMPT
"My equation predicted _____ goals. My actual was _____. The equation was (too high / too low / accurate) because _____."
Accept: slope too steep, Speed mismatch, different performance.

Say This
Open
Two matches today. Cycle is same as Day 7 — prediction before, results after. One thing changed: your prediction source is now your scatter plot equation, not the endurance equation. Plug in your Speed and read off the prediction. Written, not approximate.
Comprehension
Tell your partner: what is different about your prediction today compared to Day 7? One sentence.
Pre-Match
[Team A], what does your equation predict? [Team B], what does yours? Both numbers on paper before either bot goes on field.
Leaderboard
Most accurate scatter plot prediction today is [team]. Equation predicted [X] goals, scored [Y], gap of [Z]. Why would one team's equation be more accurate than another?
Not This
Do not revise the equation itself between rounds. Revising the prediction is correct. Changing the equation requires Open Lab.
Watch For → Redirect
Team uses endurance equation insteadAsk: 'What equation?' Redirect to y = mx + b with Speed input.
Team cannot plug in Speed or has blank equationAsk: 'What is your Speed?' Then: 'Plug that into y = mx + b. What is y?'
Team revises equation slope or interceptTell them: 'Flag this for Open Lab. Two rounds is not enough data to change the equation.'
Team treats equation output as a targetSay: 'Your equation predicts X goals. That is your prediction — not a target.'
Goal-counting disputeYour count stands. Announce before Round 1: 'Your count is final.'
Multiple teams cannot connect equation to resultDo one substitution aloud: 'Equation: y = [m]x + [b]. Speed [S]. Replace x with [S]. That is your prediction.'
Hard Stop Exit ticket (2 min) is non-negotiable. Written in data log before Rivalry Cards. Transition at 1:10 exactly.
