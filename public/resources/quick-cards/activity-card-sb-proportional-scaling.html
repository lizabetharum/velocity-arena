Reference
Motor Power = Speed × 5 (linear)
Charge Speed = Power² (quadratic)
Percent Change: (new − old) / old × 100

Say This
Open
When you add Speed, does Motor Power go up by the same percentage? More? Less?
Task
Part 1: increase Speed by 50%, calculate Motor Power for old and new. Same for Power and Charge Speed. Decide: does each scale proportionally?
Bridge
Motor Power scales proportionally with Speed. Charge Speed does not. That matters when you reallocate: a point into Power grows the output faster than a point into Speed.
Concept
Same one point. Into Speed it's a steady gain; into Power the output grows faster. Spend it where it buys the most.
Not This
Don't write 'difference = 15.' Use percent change: (45 − 30) / 30 × 100 = 50%.
