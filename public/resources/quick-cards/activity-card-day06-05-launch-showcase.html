Day 6 · Match-Build-Make · 20 min
MATH: Motor Power % = (S × 100) ÷ 20 — reverse algebra: infer input stat from behavior
Before You Start
  • Print Observation Log worksheets (one per student)
  • Write on board: OBSERVATION = what you saw + the stat it implies
  • Have timer visible and each team's stat card at their desk
  • Review Monitoring Lens to identify Level 3 and Level 4 observations
  • Prepare Part 2 for comprehension check
  • *Builds inference: students work backward from behavior to the stat—reverse algebra. Push every student from 'They were fast' to 'Speed is probably above 8.'*
Students Produce
  • Part 1: five-step sequence (locked at 2:00)
  • Part 3: observation log with team name, behavior, implied stat, confidence
  • Part 4: one adjustment with citation (team name + behavior)
  • Exit: one sentence naming rival team, behavior, stat implied
Reference
OBSERVATION LEVELS:
Level 1: Description only — press once
Level 2: Stat named, no value — push to narrow range
Level 3: Stat + range inference — valid
Level 4: Specific value + formula connection — exemplar
FORMULA: (S × 100) ÷ 20
S=5 → 25% | S=8 → 40% | S=10 → 50%
Timing
0:00–0:02Sequence CommitFill Part 1 with five steps. Read Part 2 aloud. At 1:30 warn 'thirty seconds left.' At 2:00 lock: 'Pencils down.'
0:02–0:16Showcase RunsOne team at a time. After each run, 30-sec pause: students write one observation in Part 3 (team name, behavior, stat/number, confidence). Use Monitoring Lens to note student levels.
0:13Check PaceIf behind, cut pause to 20 sec. If ahead, ask: 'Give me a range for Speed. What number?'
0:16–0:19Share-OutSelect Level 3 first, then Level 4. Revoice and name the practice: 'working backward from evidence through a formula.'
0:19–0:20ClosingRestate: every behavior is formula output; you ran it in reverse. Keep worksheets for Day 15.
0:20–0:30Adjust (10 min)ONE change per team. Part 4 requires citation: team + behavior. No citation = original stands. At 9:00 say 'Two minutes.' At 10:00 lock. Exit: back of Part 4.
Say This
Open
Commit to your sequence, watch every team run theirs, make one informed change. You are now scouting: explain what caused what you saw.
Standard
Read Part 2 silently. Turn to partners: what is missing from the NOT THIS example? What would make it YES THIS?
Showcase
An observation connects what you saw to a stat or number. Not: 'They went fast.' Yes: 'They reached the ball in 3 seconds, so Speed is probably above 8.'
After Run
Write one observation: team name, what you saw, what stat it implies, confidence. Thirty seconds.
Not This
Do not skip the pause. Do not re-run failed bots. Do not accept two adjustments. Press once on Level 1: 'What stat causes that? What formula?' Then move on.
Watch For → Redirect
Student writes 'They were fast.'Press: 'What stat? What formula?' [Wait 7 sec.]
Stat named, no range.Push: 'How high? If S=5, Motor Power=25%. If S=10, it's 50%. Where does this bot fall?'
Part 4 blank or no rival cited.Point to Part 3: 'Which team gave you this idea? If you can't name both, original stands.'
Two changes listed.Say: 'One variable only. Which one matters most?'
Bot fails to complete.Say: 'Write what the failure implies about their sequence.'
Summary instead of steps in Part 1.Say: 'One row per step. What is Step 1? What happens next?'
Hard Stop At 2:00: 'Pencils down. Sequence locked.' At 10:00: 'Sequences lock. What is written is your Season 1 sequence.' Collect worksheets after exit assessment.
