Day 8 · Hook Block · 20 min
Follows LO 7.1 review
MATH: Six Velocity Arena formulas
Before You Start
  • Verify correct answers and common errors for each formula type
  • Post the Day 7 leaderboard visibly for student reference
  • Build 6–8 problem cards using real leaderboard data
  • Place answer cards face down at central table; keep spares
  • Print one answer sheet per student and carry the Formula Error Monitoring Sheet
  • *The mechanism is the teaching.* Do not short-circuit it by confirming answers or giving calculations.
Students Produce
  • Completed answer sheet with all six calculations
  • Self-corrected work when answer is not in the pile
  • Exit reflection: which formula caused trouble and why
Reference
FORMULA ERROR
Motor Power %: Divide Speed by 20 without ×100
Charge Speed: Confuse P² with P×2
Follow Through: Multiply P×2 instead of 120 + (P×35)
Arc Segments: Over-calculate when answer = T
Turn Ratio: Drop the 0.2 base
Power at t=1: Skip inner parentheses
Timing

Say This
Open
Yesterday you saw your round 1 results. Every number on that board came from one of the six formulas. Today those same stat values are on the cards.
Task
Use the formula to get the answer. Find it in the central pile. If your answer is not in the pile, your calculation is wrong. Recalculate. Do not ask me whether your answer is right. The pile tells you.
Comprehension Check
Turn to the person next to you. Tell them: what do you do when your answer is not in the pile?
Stuck Redirect
If your answer is in the pile, it's right. If it's not in the pile, it's not.
Not This
Do not confirm answers. Do not give calculations. Do not announce copying.
Watch For → Redirect
Student asks 'Is this right?'Say: 'If your answer is in the pile, it's right. If not, it's not.' Move away.
Student copyingQuietly ask: 'Walk me through that calculation.' If they cannot, send them back to recalculate.
Student stuck 3+ minutesAsk: 'Which formula is on the back? Say it out loud. Now substitute the stat value.'
Answer card missingCheck the floor. Use spare from behind table. Resolve after activity.
Most finishing earlyAdd challenge: 'Start again with a different team's stat values. What changes?'
Most running behindDo not give answers. Use indexed formula prompt for most-common error. Move to Wrap when ready, not by clock alone.
Hard Stop Before you move: look at the leaderboard. Every stat value on the cards came from a real team. When you find your answer, you are calculating the same output that determined that team's performance. Now go.
