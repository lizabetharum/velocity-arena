Failure Bow
1 of 7
Day 2 · Hook · 15 min
MATH: Endurance formula (P(t) = 100 − (20 − Endurance) × 1.5 × t) — psychological safety before algebraic complexity
Before You Start
  • Clear floor space for full group standing circle
  • Choose and practice your first bow (Option A, B, or C) — do not improvise
  • Prepare timer (phone/watch) for 30-second rounds
  • Gather index cards or scrap paper for exit-word writing
  • Optional: marker or object to drop if using Option C
  • *If your first bow is timid, the whole activity collapses. Your theatrical commitment is the variable that determines whether the room follows.*
Students Produce
  • One public failure bow (real, fake, big, or small)
  • Unconditional cheering for every peer bow
  • One-word reflection on how the room feels (written, not collected)
Reference
FAILURE BOW OPTIONS:
A: Confident wrong answer (2+2=7)
B: Mispronunciation (En-DOOR-ance)
C: Physical drop (marker/book)
FREEZE PROMPT: 'You've got 30 seconds. Drop something. Say a wrong answer. Whatever you've got.'
REFUSAL: Say 'That's fine — next' and move on immediately.
Timing
0:00Transition InPush chairs in. Stand in loose circle. Signal once, calmly, if anyone stays seated.
0:01Facilitator's First BowDeliver your chosen bow (A/B/C) with full theatrical commitment. Plant feet, wide arms, deep bend, 2-second hold. Start cheering if needed.
0:03Rounds Begin30 seconds per person. Unconditional cheering for every bow — whisper gets same volume as pratfall. No praise specificity. Move immediately.
0:08Mid-Rounds CheckIf room is slow and nervous, keep 30-second pace strict. If loose and energized, maintain high energy. If no shift by round 5, trust structure and keep going.
0:12Closing ConsolidationAsk: 'What changed in this room between first and last bow?' Take 2–3 responses. Revoice: 'Room felt different — less tense, more ready?' Name the skill: you practiced being publicly wrong without collapsing.
0:14Exit Word (Hard Stop)Students write one word: how does this room feel now vs. when you walked in? 60 seconds, standing. Don't collect. Transition immediately into Slow Down, Slow Down.
Say This
Open
Push your chairs in and stand up. Spread out so you're not touching anyone. We're going to do something quick before we get into the math.
Explain
Here's how this works. Someone makes a mistake — real, fake, big, small, doesn't matter. Then they bow. Then we cheer. That's it. I'll go first.
After Bow
That's a Failure Bow. Your turn. Who wants to go next?
If Frozen
You've got 30 seconds. It doesn't have to be big. Drop something. Say a wrong answer. Whatever you've got.
Not This
That was great! or praising specific bows over others — specificity creates hierarchy.
Watch For → Redirect
Student laughs nervously instead of bowingCheer anyway. Don't call attention. Note their risk tolerance — it will reappear at stat allocation.
Very small bow (pencil drop, barely audible word)Cheer just as loudly as the biggest bow. Unconditional cheering — never create hierarchy.
Student refuses to participateSay 'That's fine — next' immediately. Move on without acknowledgment. Keep cheering for others.
Room not loosening by round 5Trust the structure. Shift often happens at round 9–10 when cumulative cheering hits critical mass.
Your first bow lands flatDon't explain. Say 'Wait — that wasn't big enough.' Do it again, larger. Model the willingness to retry.
Hard Stop Exit word is non-optional. 60 seconds standing. One word on anything. Don't collect. Move immediately into Slow Down, Slow Down with 'Good. Don't sit down.'
