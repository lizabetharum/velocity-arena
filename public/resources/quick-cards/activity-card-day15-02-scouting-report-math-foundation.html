MATH: P(t) = 100 − r × t, where r = (20 − E) × 1.5. Solve for x-intercept: t = 100 ÷ r.
Before You Start
  • Pre-fill opponent assignment cards with team name, opponent name, and stat allocation.
  • Print Scouting Report worksheet (Sections 1–2 only) — one per student.
  • Prepare whiteboard or chart paper to record x-intercept values during share-out.
  • Review the common error: r = E × 1.5 (inverted) vs. correct r = (20 − E) × 1.5.
  • *Section 1's Endurance stat feeds into Section 2's decay rate formula. Cross-reference during circulation.*
Students Produce
  • Section 1: opponent's stat allocation with percentage breakdown summing to 100.
  • Section 2: decay rate r, equation P(t), x-intercept, power table, and interpretation sentence.
Reference
MONITORING CHECKLIST
Sec 1 — % budget: 4 values sum to 100
Sec 2 Step 1 — r: uses r = (20 − E) × 1.5
Sec 2 Step 3 — x-intercept: t = 100 ÷ r
Sec 2 Step 5 — interpretation: states minute of zero power AND % at t = 3
WATCH FOR:
• r = E × 1.5 (inverted formula)
• Table values that don't match equation

Hard Stop
Read Sections 1 and 2 completely — 30 seconds. Section 1 is stat allocation. Section 2 is decay equation. You need Section 1 before starting Section 2. Do not calculate yet.
Probe
If E = 20, what does r = (20 − E) × 1.5 give? Would a bot with all Endurance last longer or decay faster?
Not This
Do not rush Section 3 early. Depth on Section 2 interpretation matters more than breadth.
Watch For → Redirect
Percentages don't sum to 100 in Section 1.Ask: 'What should they add to? Check your arithmetic.'
Team writes r = E × 1.5 instead of r = (20 − E) × 1.5.Ask: 'If E = 20, what does your formula give? Decay faster or slower?' Let them surface contradiction.
Table values don't match the equation.Say: 'Plug t = 1 into your equation step by step. Compare to table.'
Interpretation is qualitative instead of quantitative.Ask: 'What's the actual number? At what minute hits zero? How much power at minute 3? Write those numbers.'
Team finishes before 35-minute mark.Ask: 'If match lasted 4 minutes instead of 3, what happens to opponent's power? Use the equation.'
Team is stuck or confused.Say: 'Write opponent's Endurance stat. Now r = (20 − E) × 1.5. Substitute and compute r. That's your job next 2 minutes.'
