Open
Close your eyes for ten seconds and picture your Season 2 bot. Picture the four stats you chose. Picture one match where your bot did exactly what you designed it to do. Picture the formula behind that moment — the calculation that explains why it happened. Open your eyes. Everything you are about to explain came from those numbers. Your bot record is your evidence.
Key
Every student picks one formula from the tool. No two students pick the same one. Write your name and formula on the board before you start. Nine options: Speed, Endurance, Turning, Power, Budget, Percent Change, Distance, Linear Equation, and Proportional Scaling. Pick the one you know best.
Key
You have 10 minutes. Work individually. Enter your numbers from your bot record, calculate, read the walkthrough out loud to yourself, then write your plain-language explanation in the tool.
Close
Turn to the person next to you. Say: 'My formula is ___ and my answer is ___.' One sentence. Then write it on the board.
